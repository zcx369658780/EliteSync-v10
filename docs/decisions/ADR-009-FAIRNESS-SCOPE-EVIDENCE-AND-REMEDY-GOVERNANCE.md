# ADR-009: Fairness Scope, Evidence, and Remedy Governance

Status: `PROPOSED — AWAITING GPT L3 AND OWNER REVIEW`

Date: `2026-08-26`

Decision owner: Owner, after GPT L3 review

Depends on: ADR-001–008; accepted U-02/U-03/U-04/U-11 closeouts; Phase 34 Safety authority; accepted LC-02 boundaries

## Context

U-06 remains `BLOCKING`. EliteSync has accepted trust, provenance, lifecycle, Safety, and cross-context responsibility boundaries but no accepted fairness purpose/harm scope, affected-population framework, signal/proxy/missingness policy, evidence grammar, measurement prerequisites, remedy semantics, or affected-MVP fairness gate.

## Evidence discipline

### Observed / Accepted Authority

EliteSync does not determine human worth, universal desirability, guilt, or relationship success. Safety/private-interaction containment, named-purpose authority, correction cleanup, and scoped fail-closed rules remain controlling.

### Prior Conceptual Exploration

QA-FAIR-01 is proposal-only and supplies no finding, metric, threshold, or accepted population.

### Advisory Input

Track A research-methodology AI review is advisory input only, not fairness evidence, qualified assessment, empirical result, or Owner authority.

### Product Inference

Fairness must govern purpose-specific material harms with evidence-force limits, stop conditions, and remedies rather than one score.

### Proposed Product Decision

Adopt the model in `ELITESYNC_V10_U06_FAIRNESS_SCOPE_EVIDENCE_AND_REMEDIES_WORKSHOP_V0_1.md`, subject to Owner selection of its Decision Packet.

### Still Unknown / Requires Later Review

Groups, harms in fact, metrics, baselines, thresholds, sample/evidence sufficiency, statistics, legal categories, data authority, reviewer qualification, targets, Safety process standards, and implementation remain unresolved.

## Proposed decision

1. Define fairness as purpose-specific governance of unjustified material differential burden, exclusion, exposure, error, or recourse—not equal romantic outcomes or consent override.
2. Use the workshop's twelve-class harm taxonomy only as product-policy hypotheses; applicability requires later evidence/review.
3. Require purpose/harm-justified affected groups, missingness/consent cohorts, sparse/intersectional review where plausible, and individual severe-burden consideration without inventing a final legal group list.
4. Treat missingness/opt-out as no negative trait; require separate direct/proxy/sensitive-signal authority; prohibit Safety counts and historical convenience as ordinary person-quality proxies.
5. Use the six-level fairness evidence grammar. Disparity is not cause/discrimination; absence of observed disparity is not absence of harm; correlation is not cause; AI summary is not fairness evidence.
6. Require the fourteen measurement-plan prerequisites before later execution may even be considered. Plan completeness is not data, processing, study, or acceptance authority.
7. Prohibit one universal fairness score/global pass-fail metric; retain visible purpose/harm-specific evidence and conflicts.
8. Apply categorical block, hold, narrow, rollback/withdraw, and no-expansion rules to the affected use when authority/evidence prerequisites are absent; never convert bounded fail-closed state into person meaning.
9. Use bounded remedies that correct, withdraw, narrow, lower confidence, omit, clean stale effects, explain, contest, restore where authorized, stop expansion, require review, or remove a purpose from MVP.
10. Treat exposure, engagement, popularity, rejection, Match/message scarcity, reports, blocks, and product-created opportunity as non-neutral and never as person worth/guilt/trustworthiness truth.
11. Separate user relational preference/consent from system allocation. A declined Match is not a fairness finding; preference data does not automatically authorize sensitive/proxy exclusion.
12. Preserve Safety protection and containment. Fairness cannot require unwanted contact, weaken immediate protection, leak Safety evidence, or authorize Safety risk/prioritization.
13. Preserve privacy/data authority. Fairness creates no blanket exception or collection/processing authority; absent authorized evidence requires smaller scope or blocked affected use.
14. Treat the ten minimum semantics as a proposed affected-MVP fairness product-policy gate, not implementation or processing authority.

Use only these dependency classes: `PRODUCT RULE CAN BE OWNER-DECIDED NOW`, `REQUIRES LATER QUALIFIED FAIRNESS / RESEARCH-METHOD REVIEW`, `DEPENDS ON U-05 PARTICIPANT / DATA ACTIVITY AUTHORITY`, `DEPENDS ON U-07 SIGNAL / EVIDENCE STANDARD`, `DEPENDS ON U-08 SEGMENT / READINESS EVIDENCE`, `DEPENDS ON U-12 QUALITY / TARGET AUTHORITY`, `REQUIRES LATER LEGAL AUTHORITY`, and `PROHIBITED BY ACCEPTED AUTHORITY`.

## Recommended option

Accept the workshop's recommended A options as a coherent technology-neutral fairness governance baseline, while keeping U-06 `BLOCKING` until GPT L3 review, explicit Owner acceptance, incorporation, and final closeout.

Reasons:

- it preserves human authority, consent, Safety, privacy, and purpose boundaries;
- it distinguishes policy hypotheses, observed differences, specialist assessment, and Owner decisions;
- it creates bounded stop/remedy semantics without fabricating metrics or findings;
- it reduces MVP scope when evidence/authority is unavailable; and
- it keeps U-05/U-07/U-08/U-12 and legal dependencies independently blocking.

## Viable alternative

Exclude every fairness-sensitive Matching, Compatibility, explanation, analytics, or lifecycle use from MVP beyond the smallest already accepted explicit-signal and consent-bound functions. Perform no fairness measurement until separate authority exists.

Prefer this alternative if Owner wants the smallest safe MVP or the accepted gate cannot be met without unavailable data, legal, specialist, signal, segment, or target authority.

Reject the alternative if the product retains an affected promise/use whose differential harms, stop rules, or remedies remain hidden; deferral must remove the use.

## Rejection and exit conditions

Reject or revise the recommendation if it:

- treats a conceptual harm as an empirical finding;
- invents protected groups, metrics, thresholds, samples, power, legal conclusions, or data authority;
- permits a universal fairness/person-worth score;
- weakens consent, self-protection, Safety containment, or privacy;
- converts missingness, opt-out, rejection, popularity, reports, or blocks into adverse person meaning;
- lets AI advisory output become evidence or authority;
- permits bounded fail-closed state to become global person/account restriction; or
- requires implementation, Safety Operations, or processing to express the product rule.

Exit the affected MVP scope if required authority/evidence cannot be accepted before any later implementation decision.

## Alternatives rejected

- **One universal fairness score:** collapses unlike purposes, harms, denominators, and recourse.
- **Equal romantic outcome target:** attempts to control human consent and relationship outcomes.
- **Historical engagement as neutral baseline:** ignores product-created opportunity and selection loops.
- **Missingness/opt-out penalty:** converts user control into adverse character meaning.
- **Safety/report counts as trust proxy:** violates person-truth and containment boundaries.
- **Fairness as privacy exception:** invents collection/processing authority.
- **AI determines fairness:** promotes advisory generation into specialist/Owner authority.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Fairness questions become purpose-, harm-, population-, evidence-, and remedy-specific.
- Unsupported uses stop or narrow without global person judgment.
- Future evidence cannot silently outrun provenance, data authority, or claim strength.
- Safety, privacy, consent, and feedback-loop boundaries remain explicit.

### Costs and constraints

- Some MVP features may be removed while evidence and authority remain unavailable.
- Product teams cannot rely on one summary score or historic engagement proxy.
- Qualified review and separate data/legal/target authority remain prerequisites.
- This ADR supplies no measurement, operational, technical, or implementation design.
