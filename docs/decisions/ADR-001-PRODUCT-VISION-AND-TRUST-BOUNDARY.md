# ADR-001: Product Vision and Trust Boundary

Status: PROPOSED — AWAITING GPT L3 AND OWNER REVIEW

Date: 2026-08-23

Decision owners: Owner (final product authority), with ChatGPT architecture/product review

## Context

EliteSync-v10 is a product and architecture reset. EliteSync 9.x supplies historical algorithms, data knowledge, and design references but does not define future requirements or architecture.

Available competitor research shows two adjacent value patterns: self/cultural interpretation and broad social identity/discovery. Evidence is screenshot-only and cannot establish competitor outcomes. A v10 boundary is required before user journey, domain, AI, backend, data, or implementation decisions.

## Observed

- Historical EliteSync assets include questionnaires, matching concepts, MBTI, astrology, Bazi, Ziwei, profile, and conversation knowledge.
- CECE/测测 visibly combines self-interpretation, cultural tools, AI prompts, human consultation, community, and monetization surfaces.
- Soul visibly combines identity expression, public content, presence, matching/room entry points, and chat surfaces.
- Neither competitor's mechanics are EliteSync requirements, and no supplied evidence proves their matching quality or relationship outcomes.

## Inferred

- Users may need a bridge between self-understanding, candidate discovery, and a considered next relational action.
- Unlimited choice and falsely precise scores can undermine attention, agency, and trust.
- The architecture will later need explicit separation among user evidence, cultural interpretation, algorithmic hypotheses, AI explanations, and human decisions; this ADR does not design that architecture.

## Proposed Decision

1. **Purpose:** EliteSync helps relationship-minded adults allocate limited attention to a smaller number of better-informed, respectful conversations.
2. **Core problem:** decision quality under interpersonal uncertainty, not simple candidate supply.
3. **Position:** between self-understanding and relationship formation; not a public social-entertainment network, interpretation marketplace, or high-volume swipe product.
4. **Slow Dating:** bounded candidate supply and deliberate attention, with depth before replacement and real interaction as the source of new evidence.
5. **Compatibility:** a revisable multi-dimensional hypothesis. Algorithmic output is decision support; human relationship potential is not a score.
6. **AI boundary:** AI may summarize, explain, recommend questions, and support reflection. It may not guarantee outcomes, claim another person's inner state, manipulate emotion, impersonate users, conceal uncertainty, or replace human judgment and consent.
7. **Cultural boundary:** MBTI, astrology, Bazi, and Ziwei may be opt-in reflection languages. They are not scientific prediction, destiny, or deterministic ranking authority.
8. **MVP discipline:** exclude infinite browsing, public-feed/gift/live-room economies, expert marketplaces, legacy feature parity, deterministic prediction, autonomous courtship, and technical architecture work until the core value loop is validated.
9. **Authority:** acceptance of this ADR defines a product-research boundary only. It does not authorize code, schema, API, model, migration, backend, or Flutter work.

## Alternatives considered

### A. Restore the 9.x feature portfolio

Rejected because historical accumulation would substitute inherited scope for a validated v10 purpose.

### B. Build a Soul-like social identity network

Rejected as the default because feeds, presence, rooms, and public identity shift the core loop toward social inventory and engagement rather than considered relationship decisions.

### C. Build a CECE/测测-like interpretation product

Rejected as the default because self/cultural interpretation alone does not close the loop into reciprocal human learning and can invite false certainty.

### D. Build a high-volume swipe marketplace

Rejected because candidate throughput conflicts with deliberate attention and makes the success metric selection volume rather than decision quality.

### E. Let a compatibility score decide

Rejected because incomplete signals cannot determine relationship potential, and deterministic presentation would violate user agency and epistemic honesty.

## Consequences if accepted

### Positive

- Establishes a narrow reason for EliteSync to exist.
- Prevents competitor copying and 9.x feature accumulation.
- Creates explicit AI and cultural-system trust limits before architecture work.
- Gives Phase 2 a coherent journey and domain-language target.

### Costs and constraints

- Candidate volume, engagement, and short-term monetization cannot be default success proxies.
- Explanations, uncertainty, correction, consent, and respectful closure become product requirements to investigate.
- Some historical algorithms may remain unused if they do not meet evidence and trust boundaries.
- Product validation is required before technical implementation.

## Unknowns requiring follow-up

- Narrow launch segment and inclusion scope.
- Operational definition and measurement of meaningful conversation and informed progression.
- Exact introduction cadence and active-candidate limit.
- Signal classification for ranking, explanation-only use, and prohibited inference.
- Cultural-tool placement, consent, labeling, and fairness.
- Trust/safety escalation, moderation, age assurance, privacy, and mainland-China compliance.
- User correction, deletion, export, appeal, and recommendation-reset controls.
- Sustainable monetization consistent with non-manipulative product incentives.

## Validation required before acceptance

- GPT L3 product/architecture review.
- Owner decision on purpose, target user, position, Slow Dating definition, AI prohibitions, cultural boundary, and MVP non-goals.
- Subsequent user/concept research to test desirability and comprehension.

## Next phase if accepted

Phase 2: User Journey and Domain Language Workshop.

That phase should define the end-to-end trust-aware journey, critical moments, failure/recovery paths, and a shared domain vocabulary. It must still precede database, API, backend, Flutter, and implementation design.
