# ADR-012: Meaningful Conversation and Informed Progression Governance

Status: `PROPOSED — AWAITING GPT L3 REVIEW`

## Context

U-09 remains `BLOCKING`. No accepted construct validity, privacy-safe measurement, participant evidence, method validation, or private-Conversation inspection authority exists. This ADR proposes product-policy semantics and permissible-use boundaries only; it does not itself accept or complete U-09.

## Evidence discipline

### Observed / Accepted Authority

Human meaning and Relationship state belong to people, not activity thresholds or platform inference. Declaration, observed event, derived interpretation, AI output, Unknown, and Compatibility Hypothesis retain U-07 force limits. U-14 excludes affected private/shared-interaction analytics/model use and grants no telemetry authority. Safety and Relationship Feedback remain purpose-separated.

### Prior Conceptual Exploration

The proposed Product Vision and User Journey workshops offer vocabulary and hypotheses only. They are not accepted evidence, construct validation, measurement authority, or participant findings.

### Product Inference

The product can support deliberate interaction and human reflection without adjudicating whether private interaction is objectively meaningful or inferring relationship progress.

### Proposed Product Decision

Recommend the bounded decision below and Workshop Packet 1–12 = A. This remains proposed pending independent GPT L3 review.

### Still Unknown / Requires Later Review

Construct/measurement validity, participant evidence, population, comprehension/accessibility, privacy/legal basis, fairness, methods, metrics, targets, reviewer qualification, lifecycle, operations, technical facts, processing, and implementation remain unknown.

## Proposed Decision

1. Define `Meaningful Conversation` as participant-relative, context-specific learning or clarification within a consent-based human interaction. It may concern fit/difference, intent, boundaries, expectations, uncertainty, communication needs, next clarification, or whether further attention is warranted. It is not a platform verdict, shared truth, consent inference, positivity, romance, Compatibility, success, duration, intensity, or continuation requirement.
2. Define `Informed Progression` as improved human decision quality about continuing exploration, clarifying, pausing, closing, or remaining uncertain. It is not a U-13 state, state machine, inferred Relationship status, continuation preference, or mutuality finding.
3. Prohibit message/activity/telemetry measures, sentiment/emotion, AI summaries/confidence, inferred attraction/intent/psychology, continued contact, and Match/Connection persistence from automatically establishing meaning or success. Separately authorized observations prove only bounded events.
4. Apply U-07 exactly: User declaration is declaration-only; observation is event-only; derived/AI interpretation cannot upgrade evidence; Unknown has no adverse meaning; Compatibility Hypothesis remains purpose/evidence-gated. Create no Meaningful Conversation Score, truth class, reviewed-person finding, or success label.
5. Permit no default private content, semantic extraction, embeddings/features, sentiment/emotion inference, private-derived traits, generalized learning, hidden analytics, telemetry, or measurement. Any future operationalization requires separate authority and cannot bypass U-14.
6. Treat future same-User reflection only as a purpose-classified `User Declared` semantic possibility. It is not another person's truth, a public rating, reputation/desirability, Ranking input, general model-learning input, or current collection/processing authority.
7. Preserve attributed disagreement without averaging, forced symmetry, default cross-disclosure, consent inference, or adverse scoring.
8. Treat no reflection as unknown, no response as ambiguous, and silence as no evidence of low quality/value, deception, rejection intent, incompatibility, misconduct, or defect. Lower evidence only narrows, omits, or blocks the affected claim/use.
9. Keep Safety information isolated from ordinary Conversation-quality and person meaning. Immediate protection may override continuation without establishing guilt or Relationship truth; minimum-necessary directives remain enforcement-only.
10. Allow only a policy-level promise to support deliberate, explainable, consent-based conversations and reflection on learned/unknown matters. Prohibit guarantees, objective quality detection, private-message success prediction, automatic progress/psychological truth/relationship-success learning, hidden scoring, and private-content optimization/model learning.
11. Require the Workshop's seventeen-part future measurement/research gate. Checklist completeness creates no study, data, measurement, analytics, or processing authority.
12. Use only the Workshop's nine dependency classifications. A later GPT L3 acceptance may complete only the current pre-alpha product-policy construct/permissible-use boundary, not empirical validity, measurement, participant/data authority, private inspection, analytics, metrics/targets, Feedback collection, retained dependencies, or implementation.

## Viable alternative

Retain U-09 fully `BLOCKING` with no canonical Meaningful Conversation/Informed Progression semantics and no product promise until qualified evidence and authority exist.

Prefer this alternative if the platform cannot avoid adjudicating meaning, continuation bias, single-party truth, privacy/telemetry leakage, Safety contamination, or premature measurement authority.

## Rejection and exit conditions

Reject or revise the proposal if it:

- treats meaning, consent, mutuality, Relationship, success, or person value as platform-detectable;
- makes continuation superior to pause, closure, or uncertainty;
- promotes one person's reflection into another's truth or an adverse score;
- uses activity, metadata, operational telemetry, sentiment/emotion, AI, or model confidence as meaningfulness proof;
- permits private-content inspection, derived private artifacts, generalized learning, or a U-14 bypass;
- lets Safety information become ordinary quality, Compatibility, Ranking, reputation, or person meaning;
- creates a score, metric, threshold, target, validated construct, participant finding, data/processing authority, or implementation; or
- weakens missingness, correction, lifecycle, shared-rights, fairness, epistemic, or non-authority boundaries.

Exit the affected product promise or retain U-09 `BLOCKING` if the product-policy boundary cannot be expressed without unresolved authority.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Human meaning, consent, asymmetry, respectful closure, and uncertainty remain protected.
- Product language can distinguish supported reflection from platform adjudication.
- Future empirical proposals receive a visible privacy/evidence gate.

### Costs and constraints

- No objective Conversation-quality score or automatic progress state is available.
- Current product evidence cannot claim validity, meaningfulness, success, or progression outcomes.
- Future measurement requires extensive independent authority and review.
