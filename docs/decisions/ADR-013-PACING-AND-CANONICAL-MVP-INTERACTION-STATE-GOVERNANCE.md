## Evidence discipline

### Observed / Accepted Authority

Match is a bounded opportunity. Connection is reciprocal, current, and revocable. Conversation is bounded access, not Relationship proof. Relationship is human-defined and cannot be inferred automatically. Pause, reset, close, expire, correction, revocation, and supersession remain distinct. Missingness and ordinary closure create no adverse person meaning. Safety control is scoped and separate from Safety evidence/person meaning.

### Prior Conceptual Exploration

Prior Product Vision and User Journey workshops propose bounded supply and deliberate attention only. They do not validate pacing or authorize any exact value, feature, data activity, or implementation.

### Product Inference

Separate state vocabularies can preserve consent, lifecycle, access, Safety, and Relationship boundaries. Qualitative pacing invariants can constrain coercive behavior while all numeric/configuration choices remain deferred.

## Accepted Decision

1. Use separate product-policy vocabularies for introduction-supply participation, Match lifecycle, unilateral declarations, reciprocal Connection, Conversation access, ordinary closure/expiry, Safety control, and human Relationship meaning. Create no generic/global relationship-state concept. Future technical representation/display cannot erase provenance, authority, reciprocity, lifecycle, consent, visibility, correction, Safety separation, or human Relationship meaning.
2. Define `RECEIVING INTRODUCTIONS` as current permission for named-purpose future supply without Match Readiness, eligibility, candidate availability, supply priority, guaranteed supply, or person availability; `INTRODUCTIONS PAUSED` as User-controlled supply stop distinct from Identity deletion, negative evidence, punishment, or person meaning; and `INTRODUCTIONS CLOSED` as named-purpose future-participation cessation only, without deleting Identity/account, rewriting history, or implying Relationship, rejection motive, wrongdoing, or Safety meaning.
3. Preserve Match as bounded opportunity. `MATCH PRESENTED` is presentation only; `INTEREST EXPRESSED` is unilateral `User Declared` only; `PASSED` is neutral choice; `EXPIRED` is lifecycle cessation only. No one-sided state creates Connection.
4. Define `CONNECTION` as reciprocal, current, revocable agreement to explore communication and `CONNECTION CLOSED` as affected future ordinary access under that Connection no longer active under valid current lifecycle authority. Connection is not Relationship, friendship, commitment, exclusivity, permanence, off-platform consent, Compatibility proof, or consent to unrelated purposes.
5. Define `CONVERSATION OPEN` as bounded access under valid Connection/current controls and `CONVERSATION CLOSED` as that access ended. Create no success/failure/quality/Relationship state, permanence, or private-content/unrelated-processing consent.
6. Exclude platform-owned inferred Relationship stages from current MVP, including dating, progressing, serious, exclusive, committed, successful/declining/failed relationship, equivalent synonyms, scores, and similar labels. Activity, Match, Connection, Conversation, elapsed time, engagement, AI output, lifecycle state, telemetry, or technical state cannot create a Relationship stage. Any future recording proposal requires separate exact-vocabulary, declaration/confirmation, purpose, visibility, shared-rights, correction, lifecycle, comprehension/accessibility, applicable privacy/legal review, and processing authority.
7. Treat pass as neutral choice, no response as ambiguous, pause as User control, expiry as lifecycle cessation, Connection closure as future-access cessation, and Conversation closure as access cessation. None establishes motive, low interest, incompatibility, misconduct, guilt, low quality, low desirability, person defect, Ranking signal, reputation, Safety meaning, or Relationship failure. Lower or absent evidence may only narrow, omit, or block the affected claim/use.
8. Require bounded rather than effectively infinite introduction supply; User pause/closure without Identity deletion; deliberate consideration rather than throughput/session-time optimization; no punishment, person-standing reduction, or manipulative supply change for pause/pass/expiry/no-response; no scarcity/FOMO/guilt/streak/countdown/escalating pressure; bounded/non-escalatory reminders only if separately authorized; no engagement-as-consent; no bypass of current pause/restriction; and scoped Safety stopping without ordinary person meaning. THESE ARE PRODUCT-POLICY INVARIANTS. THEY ARE NOT EMPIRICALLY VALIDATED BEHAVIORAL FINDINGS.
9. Strictly defer every concrete batch size, active-candidate count, cadence, wait, expiry, timeout, reminder, inactivity, queue, rate, SLA/SLO, quality-threshold, and engagement-target value. No placeholder, EliteSync 9.x, competitor, industry, experimental, vendor, implementation, UI-example, timer, or operational-convenience value becomes authority.
10. Keep pause, reset, close, expire, correction, revocation, and supersession distinct; invalidate stale active dependent effects; require current affirmative authority to resume supply; prohibit historical state, provenance, cache, availability, prior consent, or implementation state from recreating withdrawn authority; preserve another person's independent history and rights; and retain attributable closure authority/reason where material.
11. Keep ordinary vocabulary distinct from block, report, allegation, interim protection, reviewed restriction, appeal/reversal, and Safety case state. Create no generic person-level `blocked`, `bad`, `unavailable`, or `unsafe` state that collapses distinct meanings. Minimum-necessary Safety directives may stop only affected supply, Match, Connection, or Conversation scope and transfer no Safety evidence, allegation detail, guilt, reputation, Compatibility, desirability, Ranking, or person meaning.
12. Permit only a policy promise of bounded supply, User-controlled pause/closure, reciprocal Connection before communication access, neutral lifecycle semantics, and no automatic Relationship-stage inference. Promise no exact pacing, optimality, respectful response, progression/conversion, or automatic Relationship understanding.
13. Permit only the Workshop's conditional disposition grammar. ### Controlling caveat 1 — governance name is not validated final copy

`CANONICAL GOVERNANCE STATE NAME != VALIDATED FINAL USER-FACING COPY`.

The canonical names accepted here are governance/product-policy semantic labels first. They do not prove User comprehension, accessibility, localization quality, wording desirability, or absence of misleading connotation. U-10 remains controlling. A future UI label may differ only while preserving semantic force; it must not strengthen/broaden authority, collapse distinct classes, or imply eligibility, readiness, mutuality, Relationship, or Safety meaning not present here.

### Controlling caveat 2 — closed does not imply mutual agreement

`CLOSED != MUTUAL AGREEMENT`.

`CONNECTION CLOSED` and `CONVERSATION CLOSED` mean only that affected ordinary future access is no longer active under valid current authority. They imply no mutual closure agreement, motive, rejection, incompatibility, wrongdoing, guilt, person defect, Relationship failure, Safety finding, or Safety case reason.

Where material for lifecycle, rights, correction, revocation, review, reconstruction, appeal, or stale-effect cleanup, attributable closure authority/reason remains distinct. User choice, expiry, revocation, supersession, and minimum-necessary Safety directive must not collapse into one human-meaning state. Restricted Safety evidence/reasoning must not be exposed through ordinary closure state. `CONTROL DIRECTIVE != SAFETY EVIDENCE`.

## Rejection and exit conditions

Reject or revise the proposal if it:

- creates a generic/global relationship state or collapses distinct authority classes;
- makes unilateral interest mutuality/contact consent or lets one-sided state create Connection;
- turns Connection/Conversation access into Relationship, quality, success, or permanent consent;
- infers Relationship stages from activity, time, engagement, AI, or lifecycle;
- converts pause/pass/expiry/no-response/closure into adverse person meaning or punishment;
- uses coercive scarcity, urgency, guilt, streak, countdown, escalation, or engagement-as-consent;
- selects any numeric/configuration value or promotes a default/reference value into authority;
- leaves stale active effects after correction/revocation/supersession or lets history recreate authority;
- leaks Safety evidence/person meaning through ordinary state; or
- claims participant evidence, validation, metrics/targets, processing, technical authority, downstream resolution, or implementation.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Consent, opportunity, access, human Relationship meaning, lifecycle cessation, and Safety control remain visibly distinct.
- Neutral pass, silence, pause, expiry, and closure avoid adverse person inference.
- Non-coercive pacing receives a bounded qualitative policy while unsupported values remain deferred.

### Costs and constraints

- No single convenient status can represent the product journey.
- Current policy cannot specify cadence, quantity, expiry, reminders, timeouts, targets, or operational behavior.
- Future UI, data, lifecycle, and technical work requires separate authority and must preserve these distinctions.
