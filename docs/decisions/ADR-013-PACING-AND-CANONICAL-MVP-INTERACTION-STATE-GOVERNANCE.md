# ADR-013: Pacing and Canonical MVP Interaction-State Governance

Status: `PROPOSED — AWAITING GPT L3 REVIEW`

Date: `2026-08-27`

Decision authority: GPT L3 only within Owner-delegated bounded governance after independent review

Depends on: ADR-001–012; accepted U-02/U-03/U-04/U-06/U-07/U-09/U-11/U-14 closeouts; accepted LC-02 and Phase 34 Safety boundaries

## Context

U-13 remains `BLOCKING`. Accepted authority distinguishes Match, Connection, Conversation, and Relationship; makes reciprocal current choice necessary for Connection; keeps Relationship human-defined; preserves lifecycle and Safety separation; and defines Informed Progression as human decision quality rather than a state machine. It does not yet supply an accepted canonical MVP interaction-state vocabulary or qualitative pacing policy.

The product-policy layer can propose distinct meanings and non-coercive invariants without selecting empirical/configuration values. Exact pacing values, comprehension, participant evidence, metrics, targets, processing, UI, architecture, and implementation remain unauthorized and unknown.

## Evidence discipline

### Observed / Accepted Authority

Match is a bounded opportunity. Connection is reciprocal, current, and revocable. Conversation is bounded access, not Relationship proof. Relationship is human-defined and cannot be inferred automatically. Pause, reset, close, expire, correction, revocation, and supersession remain distinct. Missingness and ordinary closure create no adverse person meaning. Safety control is scoped and separate from Safety evidence/person meaning.

### Prior Conceptual Exploration

Prior Product Vision and User Journey workshops propose bounded supply and deliberate attention only. They do not validate pacing or authorize any exact value, feature, data activity, or implementation.

### Product Inference

Separate state vocabularies can preserve consent, lifecycle, access, Safety, and Relationship boundaries. Qualitative pacing invariants can constrain coercive behavior while all numeric/configuration choices remain deferred.

### Proposed Product Decision

Recommend the decision below and Workshop Packet 1–13 = A. This remains proposed pending independent GPT L3 review.

### Still Unknown / Requires Later Review

User desirability/readiness evidence, comprehension/accessibility, exact wording, every pacing/configuration value, participant/data authority, metrics/baselines/thresholds/targets, empirical effects, legal conclusions, operational ownership, UI, technical architecture, and implementation remain unknown.

## Proposed Decision

1. Use separate product-policy vocabularies for introduction-supply participation, Match lifecycle, unilateral declarations, reciprocal Connection, Conversation access, ordinary closure/expiry, Safety control, and human Relationship meaning. Create no generic/global relationship-state concept.
2. Define `RECEIVING INTRODUCTIONS` as current permission for named-purpose future supply without readiness, eligibility, availability, or supply guarantee; `INTRODUCTIONS PAUSED` as User-controlled supply stop distinct from Identity deletion; and `INTRODUCTIONS CLOSED` as named-purpose future-participation cessation only. Pause/close creates no adverse meaning.
3. Preserve Match as bounded opportunity. `MATCH PRESENTED` is presentation only; `INTEREST EXPRESSED` is unilateral `User Declared` only; `PASSED` is neutral choice; `EXPIRED` is lifecycle cessation only. No one-sided state creates Connection.
4. Define `CONNECTION` as reciprocal, current, revocable agreement to explore communication and `CONNECTION CLOSED` as future ordinary access under that Connection ended. Connection is not Relationship, commitment, exclusivity, permanence, off-platform consent, or Compatibility proof.
5. Define `CONVERSATION OPEN` as bounded access under valid Connection/current controls and `CONVERSATION CLOSED` as that access ended. Create no success/failure/quality/Relationship state.
6. Exclude platform-owned inferred Relationship stages from current MVP, including dating, progressing, serious, exclusive, committed, successful/declining/failed relationship, and similar labels. Any future recording proposal requires separate exact-vocabulary, declaration/confirmation, visibility, shared-rights, correction, lifecycle, comprehension, purpose, privacy/legal, and processing authority.
7. Treat pass as neutral choice, no response as ambiguous, pause as User control, expiry as lifecycle cessation, Connection closure as future-access cessation, and Conversation closure as access cessation. None establishes motive, low interest, incompatibility, misconduct, guilt, quality, desirability, defect, or Relationship failure.
8. Require bounded rather than effectively infinite introduction supply; User pause/closure without Identity deletion; deliberate consideration rather than throughput/session-time optimization; no punishment for pause/pass/expiry/no-response; no scarcity/FOMO/guilt/streak/countdown/escalating pressure; bounded/non-escalatory reminders only if separately authorized; no engagement-as-consent; no bypass of current pause/restriction; and scoped Safety stopping without ordinary person meaning. These invariants are not empirically validated.
9. Strictly defer every concrete batch size, active-candidate count, cadence, wait, expiry, timeout, reminder, inactivity, queue, rate, SLA/SLO, quality-threshold, and engagement-target value. No placeholder, legacy, competitor, industry, experimental, vendor, or implementation default becomes authority.
10. Keep pause, reset, close, expire, correction, revocation, and supersession distinct; invalidate stale active dependent effects; require current affirmative authority to resume supply; prohibit history/provenance from recreating withdrawn authority; preserve another person's independent history.
11. Keep ordinary vocabulary distinct from block, report, allegation, interim protection, reviewed restriction, appeal/reversal, and Safety case state. Minimum-necessary Safety directives may stop only affected scope and transfer no evidence, guilt, reputation, Compatibility, desirability, Ranking, or person meaning.
12. Permit only a policy promise of bounded supply, User-controlled pause/closure, reciprocal Connection before communication access, neutral lifecycle semantics, and no automatic Relationship-stage inference. Promise no exact pacing, optimality, respectful response, progression/conversion, or automatic Relationship understanding.
13. Permit only the Workshop's conditional disposition grammar: after GPT L3 acceptance, U-13 may become `COMPLETE FOR CURRENT PRE-ALPHA PRODUCT-POLICY PURPOSE` solely for the semantic interaction-state and non-coercive pacing-policy blocker. It would not validate/select values, authorize participant/data/processing/telemetry/Relationship-status collection/technical work, or resolve retained dependencies.

## Alternative

Retain U-13 fully `BLOCKING`, define no canonical vocabulary or pacing promise, and omit affected state interpretation until all later evidence and authority exist.

Use this alternative if distinct consent/lifecycle/Safety/Relationship meanings or strict numeric deferral cannot be maintained.

## Rejection and exit conditions

Reject or revise the proposal if it:

- creates a generic/global relationship state or collapses distinct authority classes;
- makes unilateral interest mutuality/contact consent or lets one-sided state create Connection;
- turns Connection/Conversation access into Relationship, quality, success, or permanent consent;
- infers Relationship stages from activity, time, engagement, AI, or lifecycle;
- converts pause/pass/expiry/no-response/closure into adverse person meaning or punishment;
- uses coercive scarcity, urgency, guilt, streak, countdown, escalation, or engagement-as-consent;
- selects any numeric/configuration value or promotes a default/reference value into authority;
- leaves stale active effects after correction/revocation/supersession or lets history recreate authority;
- leaks Safety evidence/person meaning through ordinary state; or
- claims participant evidence, validation, metrics/targets, processing, technical authority, downstream resolution, or implementation.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Consent, opportunity, access, human Relationship meaning, lifecycle cessation, and Safety control remain visibly distinct.
- Neutral pass, silence, pause, expiry, and closure avoid adverse person inference.
- Non-coercive pacing receives a bounded qualitative policy while unsupported values remain deferred.

### Costs and constraints

- No single convenient status can represent the product journey.
- Current policy cannot specify cadence, quantity, expiry, reminders, timeouts, targets, or operational behavior.
- Future UI, data, lifecycle, and technical work requires separate authority and must preserve these distinctions.
