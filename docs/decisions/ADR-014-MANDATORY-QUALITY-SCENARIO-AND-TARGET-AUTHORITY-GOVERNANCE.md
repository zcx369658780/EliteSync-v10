### Controlling caveat 1 — portfolio coverage is not universal applicability

`MANDATORY PORTFOLIO != EVERY FAMILY APPLIES TO EVERY SCOPE`.

The seven families are mandatory governance coverage classes. Every concrete purpose, feature, design claim, review scope, product promise, or consequential use requires explicit applicability determination from purpose, information class, interaction, authority, provenance, lifecycle, harm/control path, and Safety/Privacy/Fairness boundaries. Applicable families may not be waived; genuinely non-applicable families need not be fabricated; non-applicability cannot bypass an affected quality obligation. This creates no automated applicability engine or implementation authority.

### Controlling caveat 2 — targets are exact-scope authority only

`ACCEPTED TARGET != PORTABLE UNIVERSAL DEFAULT`.

Any future accepted target is authoritative only within its exact acceptance record binding scenario/instance, purpose, population, environment, criterion/unit, evidence provenance/quality/limitations/uncertainty, method, proposer/reviewer/acceptance authority, applicable privacy/data/fairness/missingness/accessibility/comprehension/Safety/legal implications, material operational/technical context, remedy, stop/review, expiry/revalidation, and downstream scope.

No target may be silently inherited, generalized, copied, promoted, benchmarked, defaulted, or made universal across purposes, populations, environments, scenario instances, product stages, technical contexts, jurisdictions, versions, or time. Cross-scope reuse requires explicit applicability review and separately recorded authority.

## Rejection and exit conditions

Reject or revise the proposal if it:

- treats mandatory family or policy invariant as empirical validation;
- creates an `ACCEPTED TARGET` without separate evidence/review/authority;
- smuggles a value through placeholder, default, history, vendor, benchmark, AI, sample, UI, or technical convenience;
- treats delivery/retry/availability as success when current authority requires cessation;
- lets Explainability/Fairness/Auditability override Privacy/Safety/User Control;
- makes fail-closed global person/account meaning;
- creates broad private/Safety disclosure, indefinite retention, blame, guilt, reputation, or hidden score;
- silently waives an applicable mandatory family;
- lets U-15 or implementers manufacture targets; or
- claims research/data/measurement/testing/operations/design/implementation/downstream authority.
