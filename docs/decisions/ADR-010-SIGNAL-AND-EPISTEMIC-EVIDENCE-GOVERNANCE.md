## Evidence discipline

### Observed / Accepted Authority

- User declaration is not objective truth; AI interpretation is not verified fact.
- Product-observed events prove only bounded events and contexts.
- A Compatibility Hypothesis is uncertain decision support, not probability, prediction, Safety clearance, diagnosis, destiny, or worth.
- Purpose, provenance, lifecycle, correction, fairness, privacy, and Safety boundaries remain controlling.
- Missingness, opt-out, revocation, exposure, engagement, popularity, rejection, reports, blocks, and case state cannot become unsupported adverse person meaning.

### Prior Conceptual Exploration

The Compatibility Evidence Workshop supplies proposal-only signal taxonomy and evidence-language exploration. It is not a validated field allowlist or accepted U-07 standard.

### Advisory Input

Track A research-methodology AI material is advisory input only, not evidence, specialist assessment, empirical finding, UNKNOWN disposition, or Owner authority.

### Product Inference

A non-collapsing epistemic vocabulary and purpose-specific eligibility/use contract are necessary to prevent declarations, observations, interpretations, and AI language from silently acquiring stronger truth or Ranking force.

## Accepted Decision

EliteSync will apply the following class-level product-policy governance:

1. Use eight non-collapsing epistemic classes: `User Declared`, `Product Observed Event`, `Externally Supported / Verified Fact`, `Derived Interpretation`, `AI-Generated Interpretation / Explanation`, `Compatibility Hypothesis`, `Reviewed Finding`, and `Unknown / Insufficient Evidence`.
2. Treat each class as a source/force boundary, not a numeric truth hierarchy. No class may be promoted merely because information is structured, repeated, stored, available, generated by AI, or historically used.
3. Require every candidate signal to preserve source/provenance, author/observer class, named purpose, epistemic status, freshness, uncertainty/evidence strength, sensitivity/privacy, lifecycle status, permitted consumer/use, correction/revocation, and dependent-output invalidation.
4. Require claim wording and force never to exceed the strongest accepted evidence. Unsupported causal, predictive, diagnostic, deterministic, moral-character, private-thought, guilt, destiny, or relationship-success language remains prohibited.
5. Require the workshop's eleven-part candidate signal eligibility gate before any future signal class or item supports an ordinary-domain purpose. Checklist completeness does not validate an item or authorize data/processing.
6. Adopt the class-level MVP envelope in the workshop: current explicit intent and boundaries remain review-gated candidates; concrete preferences and questionnaire items remain unapproved/deferred; personality totals/type labels and cultural systems remain excluded from MVP Ranking; passive behavior, private Conversation, Safety information/counts, unsupported sensitive inference, and unauthorized scraped data remain prohibited for the stated ordinary uses.
7. Keep Ranking/Matching presentation, Explanation, optional Reflection, User Control/pacing, same-User Relationship Feedback learning, and Safety processing as non-inheriting use classes. Explanation or Reflection eligibility does not create Ranking eligibility.
8. Preserve declaration-only force for self-report, bounded-event force for observation, purpose-classified/non-reputational force for Relationship Feedback, and isolated Safety meaning.
9. Keep AI supporting-only: it may later summarize permitted information, explain bounded hypotheses, expose uncertainty, suggest questions, and identify contradiction without deciding truth, but cannot upgrade evidence, create purpose/consent, infer private thought, create a Safety/relationship finding, or generate a new Ranking signal.
10. Handle missing, stale, conflicting, weak, or unauthorized evidence by omission, lower confidence, uncertainty disclosure, narrower purpose, affected-use block, review routing, or preserved attributed positions—never adverse person meaning or permission to infer more.
11. Require source correction/supersession, stale dependent-effect invalidation/withdrawal, and separate renewed-use/recomputation authority. Provenance and caches cannot preserve or recreate unauthorized active effect.
12. Require separate review for every new signal, item, consumer, use class, or purpose, using the twelve-part expansion contract in the workshop. Analytics/model convenience creates no authority.
13. Use only these dependency classes: `PRODUCT RULE CAN BE OWNER-DECIDED NOW`, `REQUIRES LATER QUALIFIED RESEARCH-METHOD / FAIRNESS REVIEW`, `DEPENDS ON U-05 PARTICIPANT / DATA ACTIVITY AUTHORITY`, `DEPENDS ON U-08 SEGMENT / READINESS EVIDENCE`, `DEPENDS ON U-12 QUALITY / TARGET AUTHORITY`, `REQUIRES LATER LEGAL AUTHORITY`, and `PROHIBITED BY ACCEPTED AUTHORITY`.
14. Treat the eleven minimum semantics in the workshop as the Owner-accepted class-level affected-MVP signal/evidence product-policy gate. This gate is not field approval, validation, processing, Ranking design, or implementation authority.
15. Record this Owner acceptance in both authorized artifacts while keeping U-07 `BLOCKING` until final GPT L3 / Owner closeout review is completed.

### Accepted epistemic-class caveat

Epistemic class names are governance semantics first. They are not default user-facing truth labels, do not themselves strengthen evidence, and create no independent or new authority.

- For `Externally Supported / Verified Fact`, `Verified` applies only to the exact claim actually supported by separately authorized evidence. It remains bounded to the accepted source, purpose, time, and scope. It does not imply that the User as a whole is verified and does not imply verified character, stable trait, future conduct, unrelated-context truth, or perpetual truth. It remains correctable, expirable, and supersedable and creates no unrelated-purpose or cross-context authority.
- `Reviewed Finding` remains bounded to its separately authorized review purpose and scope. It does not automatically create Owner acceptance beyond the explicit recorded product decision, Ranking authority, ordinary-domain authority, cross-context authority, or universal person truth.

## Accepted current class-level envelope

- **Review-gated candidates:** current explicit user-authored intent, boundaries, and purpose-relevant interests/values.
- **Deferred and unapproved:** concrete preferences; item-level questionnaire answers; Relationship Feedback learning; every concrete field/item/use matrix.
- **Prohibited for MVP Ranking:** MBTI/personality type, questionnaire total/opaque scores, astrology, Bazi, Ziwei, zodiac/similar cultural systems, and passive behavior/activity metadata.
- **Prohibited for ordinary signal use:** private Conversation default extraction; Safety evidence/counts/case state; person-worth/popularity/rejection ground truth; inferred sensitive traits without separate authority; unauthorized external/scraped data.

No bullet above is a field-level allowlist or validation finding.

## Viable alternative

The Owner did not select this alternative.

Limit MVP evidence to attributed display of current explicit intent and boundaries, with no affected Ranking, questionnaire, cultural/personality, behavior-derived, AI-generated, or Feedback-learning use. Remove any product promise that depends on excluded signal classes.

Prefer this alternative if the Owner wants the smallest pre-alpha evidence surface or if the proposed gate cannot be met without unavailable authority. Reject it if the product retains an affected use or claim whose provenance, force, correction, or stop semantics would remain hidden.

## Rejection and exit conditions

Reject or revise the recommendation if it:

- collapses epistemic classes or treats them as numeric certainty;
- treats declarations, observed events, interpretations, AI output, historical correlation, or availability as stronger evidence than authorized;
- creates a concrete field allowlist, preference approval, questionnaire item, weight, score, feature, embedding, model input, or Ranking rule;
- allows use authority to inherit across Ranking, Explanation, Reflection, Control, Feedback, or Safety;
- weakens fairness category, missingness, privacy, consent, Safety isolation, or correction boundaries;
- permits stale or withdrawn evidence to retain active dependent effect;
- promotes Track A advisory material into evidence or authority; or
- requires study, processing, implementation, or Safety Operations to express the product rule.

Exit the affected MVP use if required authority/evidence cannot be accepted before any later implementation decision.

## Alternatives rejected

- **One universal evidence/truth score:** collapses unlike sources, purposes, uncertainty, and correction behavior.
- **All self-report is factual:** confuses current declaration with objective trait or future conduct.
- **Observed engagement is ground truth:** ignores product allocation, context, and feedback loops.
- **Explanation eligibility implies Ranking eligibility:** silently expands purpose and consequence.
- **AI-generated interpretation becomes a feature:** lets generation bootstrap evidence and signal authority.
- **Safety/report history becomes trust or Compatibility signal:** violates Safety containment and person-truth boundaries.
- **Historical EliteSync 9.x use proves validity:** replaces v10 evidence review with availability.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Signals and claims gain explicit provenance, force, purpose, lifecycle, and invalidation semantics.
- Unsupported certainty and hidden purpose inheritance become visible stop conditions.
- Cultural/personality reflection, declarations, observation, AI, feedback, and Safety remain meaningfully separated.
- Later item-level review receives a product contract without receiving processing or implementation authority.

### Costs and constraints

- MVP signal breadth remains narrow and many concrete items remain unavailable.
- Ranking cannot reuse historical weights, passive behavior, private messages, or opaque scores.
- User-facing wording, comprehension, fairness, validity, quality, and operational evidence still require later authority and review.
- Some product promises must be removed rather than implemented with insufficient evidence.
