### 2.1 Purpose-specific fairness scope

- Fairness is purpose-specific governance of unjustified material differential burden, exclusion, exposure, error, or recourse.
- Fairness does not mean equal romantic outcomes, guaranteed Matches, equal personal preferences, human-worth scoring, universal desirability, forced reciprocal access, or consent/boundary override.
- The twelve-class harm taxonomy is product-policy vocabulary only. It is not evidence that any harm occurred.
- Affected-group/person analysis requires a named purpose and harm rationale. Arbitrary or post-hoc segmentation is not accepted.
- Severe individual burden may require review even when aggregate evidence does not reveal it.

### 2.2 Signals, missingness, and adverse meaning

- Missing, unknown, non-response, opt-out, revocation, and withdrawal must not become adverse person meaning, lower worth, desirability, Compatibility, or trustworthiness.
- Lower evidence may justify only lower confidence, omission, narrower use, or a blocked affected use.
- Direct signals require separate accepted purpose and evidence authority.
- Inferred, proxy, or sensitive signals require separate necessity, purpose, fairness, privacy, evidence, and, where applicable, legal authority.
- Historical availability, historical correlation, and EliteSync 9.x weights are not v10 evidence.
- Safety reports, blocks, allegations, case states, actions, and counts must not become ordinary-domain guilt, reputation, trustworthiness, Compatibility, Ranking, desirability, or worth proxies.

### 2.3 Evidence and measurement grammar

- Conceptual risk is not observed harm.
- Observed disparity is not discrimination or causation.
- No observed disparity is not evidence of no harm.
- Correlation is not causation.
- AI summary is not fairness evidence.
- Qualified review is not Owner acceptance.
- An Owner-accepted finding remains bounded to its accepted purpose, population, time, evidence, and limitations.
- Every future fairness measurement plan must satisfy the accepted fourteen-item prerequisite contract before execution may even be considered.
- Plan completeness creates no study, data, or processing authority.
- One universal fairness score or global fairness pass/fail metric is explicitly prohibited.
- Any future evidence portfolio must remain purpose- and harm-specific and preserve conflicts, uncertainty, and unknowns rather than recombining them into one person/group truth.

### 2.4 Bounded stop and remedy semantics

- Categorical block, hold, narrow, rollback, withdraw, and no-expansion semantics apply only to the affected product purpose or use.
- A bounded fairness fail-closed state must not become global person/account meaning, guilt, risk, defect, worth, or reputation.
- Bounded remedy classes are correction, withdrawal, narrowing, confidence reduction, omission, stale-effect cleanup, renewed-use review, explanation or uncertainty improvement, applicable contest/control, authorized restoration, stop-expansion, qualified review, or MVP exclusion.
- Remedies do not promise equal human or romantic outcomes, override another person's consent, expose protected information, rewrite human memory or history, or create public blame/guilt labels.

### 2.5 Feedback, preference, Safety, and privacy boundaries

- Exposure, engagement, popularity, response, rejection, Match/message scarcity, and product-created opportunity are not neutral ground truth or person-worth/Compatibility evidence.
- Relationship Feedback remains purpose-classified.
- Private Conversation remains excluded from default fairness or model-learning use.
- User relational preference, consent, decline, and boundary are distinct from system allocation authority.
- A User declining a Match or exercising a boundary is not itself a fairness finding.
- User preference data does not automatically authorize system-level sensitive/proxy exclusion or human-worth Ranking.
- Fairness must not weaken block, decline, self-protection, or minimum-necessary immediate Safety protection.
- Fairness must not require unwanted contact or symmetrical exposure.
- Fairness review must not convert a report, block, case, or interim action into guilt or person risk.
- Ordinary fairness or Compatibility analysis receives no Safety evidence or Safety reasoning.
- Future Safety-only prioritization/risk remains `NOT DECIDED` and is not authorized by U-06.
- Fairness creates no blanket privacy, consent, purpose-limitation, minimization, retention, shared-information, or sensitive-data exception.
- If required fairness evidence cannot be obtained under accepted data authority, the affected use must remain blocked or be narrowed. Lack of evidence authority is not permission to collect more.
