## 1. Readiness state model

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `READY` | The dimension is fully populated, verified, within authority, and has no active stop condition |
| `PARTIAL` | Requirements or boundaries exist, but one or more execution inputs or approvals remain absent |
| `BLOCKED` | A required authority/input is absent or a stop condition is active; execution must not begin |
| `NOT APPLICABLE` | The dimension is demonstrably outside the exact package scope |

No dimension may be promoted based on template completeness, AI output, silence, recommendation, or inferred Owner intent.

## 2. Common readiness requirements

Before any authorized package may begin specialist review, all of the following must be `READY`:

1. **Reviewer readiness:** named reviewer identity; verifiable qualification and competence evidence; conflicts and limits; exact authority source; and scope fit.
2. **Scope readiness:** final authorized questions, linked UNKNOWNs, non-objectives, exclusions, allowed activities, forbidden activities, confidentiality boundary, and expiry/revisit conditions.
3. **Evidence readiness:** enumerated Owner-approved corpus; exact provenance and locators; checked dates; applicability, correction/retraction, conflict, transfer, and confidentiality checks; human verification; and no restricted input.
4. **Governance readiness:** exact Owner authorization binding reviewer and work; explicit contact, engagement, commissioning/spend, and disclosure levels; artifact authorship/custody/review/acceptance ownership; GPT L3/Owner review of applicable proposed governance; and stop/escalation authority.
5. **Stop-condition readiness:** complete stop rules, named actor responsible for stopping, and no active stop condition.

### 2.1 Artifact ownership requirement

The expected artifact is a `Specialist Review Record`. A later exact authorization must separately identify:

- the specialist author accountable for the bounded findings;
- the project custodian authorized to receive and preserve the record;
- the human verifier for provenance and completeness;
- the GPT L3 independent review gate;
- the Owner as the only authority for acceptance, disposition, and downstream project-state changes;
- confidentiality, access, retention, correction, expiry, and supersession responsibility.

No such execution ownership assignment is currently populated. AI tools may assist with preparation records only and cannot author or own specialist findings.
