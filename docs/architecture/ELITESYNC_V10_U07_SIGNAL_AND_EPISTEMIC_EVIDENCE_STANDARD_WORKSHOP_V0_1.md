- ADR-001–009 and the accepted U-02/U-03/U-04/U-06/U-11 closeouts are controlling upstream product-policy authority.
- User declaration is not objective truth. AI interpretation is not verified fact.
- A Compatibility Hypothesis is pair-specific, dimension-specific, uncertain, explainable, correctable, rejectable, and revisable. It is not a probability of relationship success, Safety clearance, diagnosis, destiny, or human-worth judgment.

## 1. Frozen upstream invariants

1. Product Truth → Domain → Information → Architecture → Implementation remains the refinement order.
2. Compatibility Hypothesis ≠ Match ≠ Connection ≠ Conversation ≠ Relationship.
3. Data existence, storage, visibility, custody, prior use, popularity, engagement, technical availability, historical correlation, or EliteSync 9.x use creates no evidence or purpose authority.
4. Cross-context use requires affirmative named-purpose authority. Use authority never inherits automatically across purposes.
5. Missing, unknown, opt-out, non-response, revocation, or withdrawal creates no adverse person meaning.
6. Lower evidence permits only lower confidence, omission, narrower scope, or a blocked affected use.
7. Fairness evaluation categories remain bounded evaluation semantics and cannot become User attributes, product signals, Ranking features, public labels, or unrelated-purpose authority.
8. U-06 Packet 11 A approves governance conditions only and no concrete preference field, sensitive/proxy preference, signal allowlist expansion, Ranking feature, weight, inference, model, or implementation.
9. Safety evidence, counts, allegations, cases, actions, and reasoning remain excluded from ordinary Compatibility, Matching, Ranking, reputation, desirability, or worth.
10. Private Conversation remains excluded from default signal extraction. Relationship Feedback remains purpose-classified.
11. Correction, revocation, reversal, and supersession remove stale dependent active effects. Provenance cannot preserve unauthorized active use.
12. AI and analytics are supporting capabilities only and cannot create truth, consent, guilt, Connection, Relationship state, Safety findings, or new signal/purpose authority.

## 2. Canonical epistemic-status vocabulary

The following classes are non-collapsing. They describe source and permitted force, not a numeric truth hierarchy.

| Epistemic status | Can establish | Cannot establish | Maximum accepted product-policy force | Correction / supersession | Prohibited promotion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `User Declared` | What a User currently authored or stated in a named context | Objective trait, future conduct, honesty, another person's state | Descriptive statement; candidate use only under a separately accepted purpose gate | User correction or withdrawal supersedes future optional active use | Repetition or structured form cannot convert it into verified fact |
| `Product Observed Event` | That the bounded product event occurred in the recorded context | Motive, private thought, stable trait, attraction, worth, intent, or causal meaning | Bounded event description or separately authorized control adaptation | Correct event error; expire or withdraw stale dependent use | Frequency, engagement, or availability cannot create trait or Ranking truth |
| `Externally Supported / Verified Fact` | Only the exact claim supported by separately authorized evidence and verification | User-as-a-whole verification, character, stable trait, future conduct, unrelated-context truth, or perpetual truth | Bounded factual claim for the accepted source, purpose, time, and scope | Correct, expire, or supersede when source/authority changes | The label is prohibited unless verification authority and evidence actually exist; it creates no unrelated-purpose or cross-context authority |
| `Derived Interpretation` | A transparent rule- or analyst-derived tentative meaning from permitted sources | New fact, consent, cause, diagnosis, destiny, or private thought | Tentative explanation or reflection; Ranking only after separate item/use review | Invalidate when source, rule, purpose, or authority changes | Derivation cannot upgrade source evidence strength |
| `AI-Generated Interpretation / Explanation` | That AI generated a bounded summary, explanation, question, or tentative interpretation | Verified fact, reviewed finding, probability, consent, guilt, Safety state, or new signal | Clearly labeled explanation/reflection under separate authority | Dismiss, correct, regenerate, or withdraw after source/authority change | Fluency, repetition, or model confidence cannot upgrade evidence |
| `Compatibility Hypothesis` | A pair- and dimension-specific, evidence-bounded proposition for human exploration | Match outcome, success probability, destiny, Safety clearance, diagnosis, or worth | Explainable, uncertain decision support only | Revise, reject, withdraw, or invalidate with evidence/lifecycle change | Must never become scalar person or relationship truth |
| `Reviewed Finding` | Only the bounded finding established through a separately authorized qualified review | Owner acceptance beyond the explicit recorded product decision, universal person truth, ordinary-domain meaning, or permanent person meaning | Consequences only within the separately authorized review purpose and scope | Correct, reverse, supersede, or expire under the governing review authority | Review status creates no Ranking, ordinary-domain, or cross-context authority |
| `Unknown / Insufficient Evidence` | That evidence is unavailable, stale, conflicting, weak, or outside authority | Negative trait, guilt, risk, deception, incompatibility, or permission to infer more | Omit, disclose uncertainty, narrow, block, or route for review | May change only with newly authorized evidence or correction | Unknown must never be promoted into adverse person meaning |

Accepted dependency classes: `PRODUCT RULE CAN BE OWNER-DECIDED NOW`; `REQUIRES LATER QUALIFIED RESEARCH-METHOD / FAIRNESS REVIEW`; `DEPENDS ON U-05 PARTICIPANT / DATA ACTIVITY AUTHORITY`; `DEPENDS ON U-08 SEGMENT / READINESS EVIDENCE`; `DEPENDS ON U-12 QUALITY / TARGET AUTHORITY`; `REQUIRES LATER LEGAL AUTHORITY`; `PROHIBITED BY ACCEPTED AUTHORITY` where the affected use would cross an accepted prohibition. These are the only permitted dependency classes.

### Accepted epistemic-class caveat

Epistemic class names are governance semantics first. They are not default user-facing truth labels, do not themselves strengthen evidence, and create no independent or new authority.

- For `Externally Supported / Verified Fact`, `Verified` applies only to the exact claim actually supported by separately authorized evidence. It remains bounded to the accepted source, purpose, time, and scope. It does not imply that the User as a whole is verified and does not imply verified character, stable trait, future conduct, unrelated-context truth, or perpetual truth. It remains correctable, expirable, and supersedable and creates no unrelated-purpose or cross-context authority.
- `Reviewed Finding` remains bounded to its separately authorized review purpose and scope. It does not automatically create Owner acceptance beyond the explicit recorded product decision, Ranking authority, ordinary-domain authority, cross-context authority, or universal person truth.

## 3. Signal provenance and authority contract

Before any candidate signal can support a named use, its policy record must state:

1. source and provenance;
2. author or observer class where relevant;
3. named purpose;
4. epistemic status;
5. time relevance and freshness;
6. uncertainty and evidence strength;
7. sensitivity/privacy class where applicable;
8. lifecycle authority and current status;
9. permitted consumer and use class;
10. correction and revocation behavior; and
11. dependent-output invalidation behavior.

Custody, storage, visibility, availability, prior use, analytics access, or implementation convenience is not evidence, purpose, lifecycle, or consumer authority. An incomplete contract blocks the affected use; completeness itself creates no study, data, processing, or implementation authority.

## 4. Evidence-to-claim proportionality

The strength, specificity, and wording of a claim must not exceed the strongest accepted evidence supporting it.

| Claim class | Permitted force | Required boundary |
|---|---|---|
| User-authored description | “The User currently states…” | Preserve context, time, correction, and non-verification |
| Tentative interpretation/reflection | “This may suggest…” / question-form reflection | Expose provenance, uncertainty, alternatives, and dismissal |
| Compatibility Hypothesis | “These permitted signals may support or complicate exploring…” | Pair/dimension scope; no scalar certainty or outcome prediction |
| Bounded product-decision explanation | Explain the permitted sources and rules used for that affected decision | No hidden source, purpose inheritance, or person-worth meaning |
| Reviewed factual finding | State only the exact later accepted finding | Preserve population, purpose, time, reviewer authority, evidence, and limitations |

Causal, predictive, diagnostic, deterministic, moral-character, private-thought, guilt, destiny, or relationship-success language is prohibited unless separately accepted evidence and authority truly support that exact claim; current authority supplies none.

## 5. Candidate signal eligibility gate

Every signal class and every future concrete item must pass all of the following before supporting an ordinary-domain purpose:

1. named purpose and direct relevance;
2. source/provenance clarity;
3. epistemic-status and evidence-strength classification;
4. user comprehensibility appropriate to the claim;
5. correction and revocation support;
6. lifecycle authority;
7. privacy/sensitivity review where applicable;
8. fairness/proxy/missingness review where applicable;
9. no accepted prohibition;
10. bounded explanation semantics; and
11. stop/review behavior for missing, stale, conflicting, or insufficient evidence.

Failing any required element blocks or narrows only the affected use. Passing this policy checklist does not validate the item, place it on a field allowlist, or authorize collection/processing.

## 7. Ranking, Explanation, Reflection, and Control-use separation

| Use class | Owner-accepted boundary |
|---|---|
| Ranking / Matching presentation | Requires the strictest named-purpose, item-level evidence, fairness, privacy, comprehension, lifecycle, and correction review; current class candidacy is not permission |
| Explanation | May explain only the permitted sources and bounded logic used; no hidden evidence or stronger truth claim |
| Optional personal reflection | May use tentative, provenance-visible, user-controlled material; cannot become Ranking authority |
| User control / pacing adaptation | May respond to explicit current choices for that User and purpose; cannot infer personality, desirability, or relationship capacity |
| Relationship Feedback learning | Same-User, named-purpose, separately authorized, correctable, non-public, and non-reputational only |
| Safety processing | Separately governed and isolated; creates no ordinary signal authority |

Eligibility for Explanation or Reflection does not imply eligibility for Ranking. Eligibility for Control does not imply a person trait. No use class inherits authority from another.

## 8. Cultural and personality evidence boundary

Maintain the ADR-003 class-level baseline: MBTI type, personality-system labels, questionnaire totals/opaque scores, astrology, Bazi, Ziwei, zodiac, and similar systems are not MVP Ranking evidence. Optional reflection/explanation requires explicit user control, provenance, tentative framing, non-scientific framing where applicable, correction/dismissal, and no destiny, diagnosis, private-thought, verified-fact, or success claim. Underlying questionnaire answers remain item-by-item candidates only after separate review.

## 9. Self-report and declaration boundary

- A declaration is authoritative as a declaration, not proof of objective trait, future conduct, honesty, or stable identity.
- Self-report is contextual, time-sensitive, correctable, revocable for future optional use, and vulnerable to ambiguity, impression management, and social-desirability effects.
- Repetition, structured format, or prior use does not increase its epistemic force.
- Missing declaration creates no adverse inference.
- Dependent active outputs must be invalidated or withdrawn when controlling declarations are corrected, superseded, or withdrawn.

## 10. Observed behavior and feedback boundary

- A product-observed event proves only that bounded event and context.
- Exposure, engagement, popularity, response latency, rejection, Match/message scarcity, and product-created opportunity are not neutral ground truth, motive, trait, worth, or Compatibility evidence.
- Product allocation can create self-reinforcing feedback and must remain visible as a source of selection.
- Passive behavior remains excluded from MVP Compatibility Ranking.
- Private Conversation remains excluded from default signal extraction.
- Relationship Feedback cannot silently become public reputation or universal desirability.
- Report, block, and Safety history cannot become an ordinary-domain signal.

## 11. AI interpretation non-authority

Under later separate authority, AI may summarize permitted information, explain a bounded hypothesis, expose uncertainty, suggest questions or reflection, identify contradiction without adjudicating truth, and generate tentative provenance-consistent language.

AI must not upgrade evidence strength; create consent, purpose, lifecycle, or consumer authority; infer private thoughts; create diagnosis, destiny, guilt, Connection, Relationship state, or Safety findings; convert weak evidence into precise probability/certainty; or create a new Ranking signal merely because a model generated an interpretation.

## 12. Missing, stale, conflicting, or insufficient evidence

Allowed safe outcomes are: omit the claim, lower confidence, show uncertainty, narrow the purpose, block the affected use, route for purpose/evidence review, or preserve multiple attributed positions without adjudicating person truth.

Unknown or insufficient evidence must not become negative person meaning, deception, guilt, risk, incompatibility, or permission to infer, collect, or process more. Scoped fail-closed behavior remains limited to the affected purpose/use.

## 13. Correction, revocation, and dependent-output invalidation

Every correction, revocation, reversal, or supersession affecting dependent use requires:

1. an authoritative source declaration;
2. cleanup, invalidation, or withdrawal of stale active dependent Compatibility, Matching, Explanation, or Control effects; and
3. a separate decision on whether renewed use or recomputation is permitted.

Provenance may preserve accountable history but cannot preserve unauthorized active effect. Caches, history, derived interpretations, summaries, or AI outputs cannot conceptually recreate withdrawn authority. Disputed positions preserve provenance and attribution rather than applying “last edit wins” or declaring person truth. This is a semantic responsibility contract, not a technical propagation design.
