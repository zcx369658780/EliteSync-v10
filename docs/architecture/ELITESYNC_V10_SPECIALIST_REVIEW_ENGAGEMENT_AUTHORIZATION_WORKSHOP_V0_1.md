## 1. Common staged authorization model

Human specialist authority must be established through separate, non-inferred stages:

```text
Owner-approved sourcing authorization
→ verified candidate and conflict/independence assessment
→ separate Owner contact authorization
→ bounded contact and fit record
→ separate Owner engagement/commissioning authorization
→ approved evidence corpus and transfer authority
→ final readiness confirmation with no active stop condition
→ specialist review execution under a later exact task
```

Permission at one stage never grants the next. Blank fields, silence, availability, referral, reputation, package completeness, AI output, GPT L3 recommendation, or prior contact do not create authority.

## 2. Reviewer sourcing governance

### 2.1 Future allowed sourcing channels

A later exact Owner authorization may permit one or more of these auditable channel categories:

- recognized professional bodies, registries, or association directories;
- verified university, research institution, regulator, standards body, civil-society, or professional organization affiliation pages;
- pre-existing referrals explicitly approved by the Owner for this package;
- formal procurement, vendor, or consultant panels under separately authorized procurement governance;
- direct Owner nomination with the same independent verification requirements as every other channel.

This list does not authorize searching or candidate identification in Phase 22. General web search, social-media discovery, scraped contact lists, data brokers, covert profiling, automated outreach, unsolicited bulk contact, AI-generated identities, and inferred personal information are not permitted sourcing methods.

### 2.2 Required sourcing authorization record

```text
Sourcing authorization ID:
Owner-authorized package/SRA:
Authorized sourcing person:
Allowed channel categories and exact systems:
Purpose and candidate-count/time bounds:
Permitted information and search terms:
Forbidden information and methods:
Qualification/identity verification standard:
Conflict and independence standard:
Confidentiality and retention boundary:
Candidate-record custodian:
Stop/escalation conditions:
Expiry:
```

Only the named human or role may source within the exact channels and limits. Agents and AI tools cannot be specialist authority, identity evidence, or the sole verifier.

### 2.3 Qualification and identity verification

Before a candidate can be proposed for contact, a named human verifier must record:

- independently attributable identity and current role;
- qualification/experience evidence, issuer, exact locator, date checked, and current status;
- competence match to the exact package, questions, population/product context, and known exclusions;
- professional standing where relevant and any unresolved verification gap;
- capacity to preserve confidentiality, provenance, limitations, and independence;
- what was not verified and the expiry/recheck date.

Self-description, model output, popularity, publication count, employer reputation, referral, or directory presence alone is insufficient.

### 2.4 Conflict and independence checks

Disclose and assess financial, employment, ownership, authorship, advocacy, personal, referral, competitive, prior/current project, litigation/regulatory, confidentiality, contingent-compensation, and outcome-dependent interests. Independence requires freedom to report contrary, negative, unresolved, and decision-reversing findings without incentive or instruction to reach a preferred result.

The assessment must record the reviewer, evidence, materiality, mitigation, residual risk, recusal decision, and Owner disposition. A sourcing person cannot self-approve an unresolved material conflict.

### 2.5 Rejection criteria

Reject or stop a candidate when identity/qualification cannot be independently verified; competence does not match; authority is misrepresented; a material conflict cannot be mitigated; independence is compromised; confidentiality/provenance requirements cannot be met; restricted data or excessive disclosure is requested; the person requires work beyond scope; compensation depends on a preferred conclusion; the person cannot preserve uncertainty or dissent; or legal/Safety/Owner/technical authority is claimed beyond the package.

Rejection is a sourcing decision only. It is not a public reputation judgment, Safety finding, professional misconduct finding, or general statement about the person.

## 3. Contact authorization governance

Contact requires a separate exact Owner authorization after candidate verification. Sourcing permission never authorizes contact.

### 3.1 Required contact authorization record

```text
Contact authorization ID:
Linked sourcing record and package/SRA:
Authorized human sender:
Exact recipient identity and role:
Approved channel/account:
Communication purpose:
Permitted questions:
Permitted disclosure and attachments:
Forbidden disclosure:
Confidentiality terms:
Response and contact-record custodian:
Contact count/time/expiry limit:
Commitment and compensation authority: NONE | exact bound
Stop/escalation conditions:
```

### 3.2 Purpose and recipient scope

Permitted future purpose may be limited to confirming identity, availability, qualification, competence fit, conflicts, independence, confidentiality capacity, interest in the bounded scope, and willingness to produce a `Specialist Review Record`. Each authorization must name the exact recipient; it does not permit contacting substitutes, colleagues, institutions, referrals, or additional candidates.

Contact must not imply selection, endorsement, engagement, commissioning, payment, review authority, compliance, legal opinion, Safety authority, UNKNOWN resolution, or future work.

### 3.3 Disclosure and confidentiality

Default permitted disclosure is the minimum public or Owner-approved non-confidential conceptual information necessary for the authorized purpose. No repository-private, personal, sensitive, private-interaction, Safety, participant, case, production, credential, confidential, or third-party-restricted material may be disclosed.

The authorization must define confidentiality terms, permitted recipients, onward-disclosure prohibition, approved storage/retention, correction/withdrawal process, and incident escalation. Unknown recipient identity, channel security, retention, training use, logging, or onward disclosure requires stopping.

### 3.4 Contact stop conditions

Stop before sending or continuing when sender/recipient/channel/purpose authority is unclear; identity or qualification conflicts; the recipient requests restricted data, repository access, credentials, production access, payment, commitment, actual review, broader scope, technical work, person/case information, or unauthorized evidence; confidentiality is unclear; a material conflict appears; or contact would create an external commitment.

## 4. Engagement authorization governance

Engagement requires a separate exact Owner authorization after contact and fit assessment. Contact, interest, availability, or a proposed fee does not grant engagement or commissioning authority.

### 4.1 Required engagement authorization record

```text
Engagement authorization ID:
Named specialist and verified authority record:
Package/SRA and exact questions:
Scope, non-objectives, exclusions, and allowed/forbidden activities:
Approved evidence corpus and access rights:
Confidentiality, security, disclosure, and incident terms:
Deliverables and acceptance criteria:
Artifact authorship, custody, verification, access, retention, and correction ownership:
Compensation/spend authority, approver, limit, payee, and terms:
Start date, end date, expiry, milestones, and refresh conditions:
Communication and escalation authority:
Termination conditions and post-termination duties:
GPT L3 review route:
Owner decision/acceptance route:
```

### 4.2 Scope and deliverables

Engagement must bind the named specialist to one exact package, question set, evidence corpus, and competence boundary. Cross-package work requires separate authorization. The authorized deliverable is a bounded `Specialist Review Record` plus only the evidence summary and UNKNOWN-impact analysis explicitly authorized for that package.

No engagement may deliver an `Expert Opinion Report` as decision authority, legal opinion unless separately required and authorized, compliance certificate, Safety action, person-level judgment, Owner decision, design artifact, implementation plan, or code.

### 4.3 Ownership and decision authority

- The specialist owns authorship responsibility for their bounded findings and corrections.
- The source issuer/author retains original source and intellectual provenance; corpus inclusion does not transfer source ownership.
- A named project custodian owns authorized receipt, integrity, access, retention, correction, supersession, and disposal administration for the record.
- A named human verifier owns provenance/completeness checking without rewriting the specialist finding.
- GPT L3 remains an independent review gate, not artifact author or Owner authority.
- The Owner alone decides acceptance, revision, rejection, deferral, UNKNOWN disposition, and downstream project-state impact.

Contractual intellectual-property ownership, license, publication, reuse, and attribution terms must be explicit before engagement. No ownership or license is inferred from payment, delivery, repository storage, or access.

### 4.4 Compensation authority

Current compensation and spend authority is `NONE`. A later authorization must name the budget/spend approver, payee, currency, amount or cap, payment basis, invoice/tax/expense terms, milestone/acceptance relationship, cancellation liability, and prohibited contingent-outcome incentives. No agent may negotiate, promise, incur, or approve spend without that exact authority.

### 4.5 Duration and termination

Every engagement must have a start date, end date or expiry, milestone/review points, evidence/source refresh triggers, and a rule for work after expiry. Terminate or suspend immediately for scope drift, authority gap, qualification/conflict change, independence compromise, confidentiality/security concern, restricted-data need, unsupported conclusion, provenance failure, non-cooperation with correction/limitations, unauthorized contact/disclosure, operational/person-level action, or entry into technical design/implementation.

Post-termination duties must cover stop-work acknowledgement, no further contact, return/deletion or authorized retention of materials, confidentiality survival, artifact status, unpaid/approved obligations, incident reporting, and supersession marking. Termination does not validate or erase prior findings automatically.

## 5. Evidence corpus governance

### 5.1 Source ownership and corpus approval

The Owner approves corpus scope for the exact package, or a later exact Owner record may explicitly delegate a named authority with bounded scope and expiry. Without that record, no other role may approve or transfer a corpus. Approval does not claim ownership of third-party sources. Source authors/issuers and applicable license/rightsholders retain ownership and rights. The corpus record must state access/license basis, permitted review use, copying/quotation limits, attribution, redistribution limits, and withdrawal requirements.

### 5.2 Provenance manifest

Before any access or transfer, enumerate each item with:

```text
Corpus/source ID:
Package/question linkage:
Issuer/author and title:
Stable locator and exact section/page:
Source class:
Publication/effective/version date:
Access and checked date:
Jurisdiction/population/product/purpose applicability:
Ownership/license/access basis:
Confidentiality/data classification:
Integrity/hash where lawful and useful:
Corrections/retractions/amendments:
Conflicts/dissent/transfer limits:
Known limitations and expiry/recheck date:
```

AI summaries remain `AI-assisted preparation`; the actual verified source is the evidence.

### 5.3 Access rights and confidentiality

Access must be least-privilege, purpose-specific, named, time-bounded, revocable, and logged where appropriate. The engagement record must name who may view, copy, annotate, quote, transfer, retain, correct, and dispose of each class. Unknown ownership, license, confidentiality, destination, tool retention, onward disclosure, or access authority requires stopping.

Phase 22 authorizes no corpus transfer or external/private-data access. Future default corpus is public or Owner-approved non-confidential conceptual material only. Personal, sensitive, private-interaction, Safety, participant, real-case, production, credential, repository-private, or confidential material requires separate exact authority and applicable governance; omission never grants it.

### 5.4 Verification responsibility

A named human verifier must check source identity, locator, version/date, integrity, applicability, ownership/access basis, corrections/retractions, conflicts, limitations, and claim mapping before transfer and before a finding is relied upon. The specialist records source-to-finding reasoning and unresolved uncertainty. The custodian preserves the manifest and change history. AI/tool output cannot be the sole verifier.

### 5.5 Retention responsibility

A later authorization must name the custodian, retention purpose and period, access-control owner, review cadence, correction/supersession process, withdrawal response, incident response, archive/disposal method, and proof of return/deletion where required. Retention must not silently broaden purpose, reuse, training, publication, analytics, or model-improvement authority.

## 6. Package-specific overlays

### 6.1 SRA-RM-01 — Research methodology

- **Sourcing competence:** Research design, construct/measurement validity, evidence synthesis, source hierarchy, uncertainty, missingness, transfer/generalization, and claim-strength calibration.
- **Engagement scope:** U-01, U-05, U-06, U-07, U-09, U-12, and U-14 methodology questions only.
- **Forbidden:** Participant research; dataset/user/private/Safety data analysis; statistical execution; experiments; model creation; metric/threshold/signal approval; privacy/legal or Safety decisions; technical design or implementation.
- **Corpus boundary:** Verified methodological and question-relevant sources; no dataset or participant material.

### 6.2 SRA-PL-01 — Privacy/legal

- **Sourcing competence:** Privacy, information rights, and data governance relevant to a later Owner-selected jurisdiction, population, entity, and purpose. `Privacy/legal specialist` is the initial role; licensed legal counsel is not required unless a later formal-legal-opinion decision says otherwise.
- **Engagement scope:** U-01, U-02, U-03, and U-14 only.
- **Forbidden:** Selecting jurisdiction; legal opinion requirement; compliance/legality/certification claim; lawful-basis assumption; personal/restricted data processing; production/legal commitment; technical design or implementation.
- **Corpus boundary:** Current official primary sources for the Owner-selected context with exact article/section, effective/version and checked dates, applicability, translation, amendment/correction, conflict, and limitation records.

### 6.3 SRA-SM-01 — Safety/moderation

- **Sourcing competence:** Online Safety/moderation policy, harm-aware conceptual scenarios, responsibility/escalation, human review, recourse, false-positive/false-negative, and capacity limits.
- **Engagement scope:** U-03, U-04, U-11, and U-12 only; conceptual, responsibility-focused, hypothetical, sanitized, and non-person-level.
- **Forbidden:** Real-person/case/report/allegation/emergency review; guilt/reputation judgment; person-level assessment/risk scoring; moderation/restriction/emergency action; external routing; Safety/private data; autonomous AI authority; technical design or implementation.
- **Corpus boundary:** Owner-approved public/non-confidential authoritative guidance and sanitized hypothetical scenarios only; no real Safety record or case data.
