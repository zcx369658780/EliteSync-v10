## 4. Accepted D01-OM-DD operational decisions

Exactly thirteen design records were accepted: D01-OM-DD-01 through D01-OM-DD-13. Each record contains exactly seventeen required fields, once each and in the required order.

### D01-OM-DD-01 — invocation identity and correlation

Every `authorConnectionClosure` boundary occurrence is a distinct operational invocation occurrence. Only an occurrence for which the existing contract boundary makes one authenticated author and one exact Connection normatively available is eligible for D-01 correlation. Correlation relates the eligible occurrence to that author, Connection, and one Connection-scoped effect decision while keeping invocation occurrence, authored closure intent, and closure effect non-interchangeable. An unavailable occurrence retains its unchanged MC-DD-09 outcome and creates no D-01 classification. Multiple eligible occurrences may correlate to the same effect decision without becoming the same occurrence or authored act. No identifier carrier, key, token, header, correlation algorithm, persistence representation, or implementation was selected.

### D01-OM-DD-02 — authenticated author and exact Connection binding

Before D-01 operational classification, the existing contract boundary must make one boundary-authenticated User and one exact MC-DD-02 Connection normatively available. The eligible invocation is bound to that pair for the complete normative decision; request content, another identity, another Connection, or a derived effect cannot replace, widen, transfer, or override it. If authentication or exact-Connection authority is unavailable, no D-01 binding or classification is produced and the unchanged MC-DD-09 outcome controls. No authentication method, credential/session model, authorization engine, lookup, persistence, or runtime enforcement was selected.

### D01-OM-DD-03 — closure-effect recognition

For the bound exact Connection, the effect decision has exactly three normative conditions: `ESTABLISHMENT_ELIGIBLE`, `ALREADY_ESTABLISHED`, or `OUTCOME_UNAVAILABLE`. `ALREADY_ESTABLISHED` means only that loss of authority for future optional continuation is the controlling effect meaning. `OUTCOME_UNAVAILABLE` authorizes no success representation and creates no opposite or reopened state. These conditions are not execution or persistence proof, and no storage, recognition algorithm, timeout, recovery method, or runtime observation was selected.

### D01-OM-DD-04 — effect cardinality and no-new-effect

For one exact Connection, effect-decision cardinality is zero before valid first establishment and exactly one after establishment. First establishment moves the permitted cardinality to one; every later eligible invocation is constrained to no-new-effect. No operation may create a second effect, broaden scope, add a dependent-effect class, reopen, or reverse the established effect. No deduplication, retry, concurrency, transaction, lock, storage, or runtime mechanism was selected.

### D01-OM-DD-05 — same-author reaffirmation

A later eligible invocation from the same authenticated author for the same exact Connection after establishment is repeated authoring with reaffirmation and no-new-effect. It preserves that author's continuing closure intent and the one effect. It creates neither another effect nor reciprocal agreement, reopening, or reversal. The decision selects no comparison algorithm, retry policy, persistence operation, or execution behavior.

### D01-OM-DD-06 — other-participant independent authorship

A later eligible invocation from the other authenticated participant for the same exact Connection after establishment is that participant's independent authored closure intent with no-new-effect. It is not same-author reaffirmation, reciprocal agreement, validation, replacement, or revision of the first author's intent or the established effect. It selects no identity-resolution, author-comparison, persistence, or execution mechanism.

### D01-OM-DD-07 — unchanged API outcome mapping

Every normatively available established-effect success—first establishment, same-author reaffirmation/no-new-effect, or other-participant independent authoring/no-new-effect—maps to the unchanged MC-DD-05 object and MC-DD-06 HTTP `200`. Every unavailable contract outcome maps only to one of the four unchanged MC-DD-09 errors. No new variant, field, status, code, route, runtime-cause taxonomy, retryability, recovery, delivery, or persistence meaning was created.

### D01-OM-DD-08 — Backend normative responsibility

Backend's target-independent normative responsibility is to preserve the author/Connection pair; obtain a D01-OM-DD-03 condition; apply D01-OM-DD-04 cardinality; preserve D01-OM-DD-05/06 authorship classification; coordinate only already-governed dependent ordinary-effect responsibility; hand off only the normative persistence-support requirement; and expose only D01-OM-DD-07 mapping. No success is normatively available while those obligations contradict one another. BE-DD-10 remains the ceiling. No controller, service, module, call order, policy engine, timing, delivery, persistence implementation, or component ownership was selected.

### D01-OM-DD-09 — Database persistence-support responsibility

Database's target-independent responsibility is one exact-Connection effect-decision support boundary able to preserve only coherent first-establishment, same-author reaffirmation/no-new-effect, other-participant independent-authoring/no-new-effect, or outcome-unavailable meanings. It must prevent a valid contradictory, partial, duplicate, broader, reopened, or reversed effect meaning and preserve prior receipt and independent authorship. DB-DD-11 remains the ceiling. This is not proof that support or a persisted fact exists, and no table, field, key, index, constraint, representation, operation, transaction, engine, retention, or migration was selected.

### D01-OM-DD-10 — effect-oriented repeat handling

Operational repeat handling uses effect-oriented re-evaluation rather than request-level suppression. Every later occurrence that independently satisfies the authenticated-author and exact-Connection boundary is evaluated against the one current normative effect decision. No occurrence is made identical to another, and request-level duplicate classification cannot erase same-author reaffirmation or other-participant independent authorship. Re-evaluation may reproduce unchanged established-effect success but cannot create a new effect. No automatic retry policy, request identity carrier, idempotency key/header/token, persistence token/key, request-deduplication algorithm, retry count/backoff, replay window, timeout, queue, or network-retry behavior was selected.

### D01-OM-DD-11 — concurrency and race semantics

All semantically eligible concurrent invocations for the same exact Connection have a sequence-independent normative result and converge on one closure effect. At most one effect moves from not established to established; every other eligible concurrent occurrence is classified as same-author reaffirmation/no-new-effect or other-participant independent authoring/no-new-effect. An invalid occurrence retains its error meaning and cannot suppress a valid occurrence. No winner, synchronization algorithm, lock, compare-and-set/version strategy, scheduler, transaction, isolation level, or storage engine was selected.

### D01-OM-DD-12 — normative atomic consistency

One semantically eligible invocation is one normative per-Connection consistency unit. Within it, author/Connection binding, effect condition, authorship classification, zero-or-one cardinality, `futureOptionalContinuation = NOT_AUTHORIZED`, and the normatively available API outcome must be mutually consistent. No valid unit may expose partial, contradictory, duplicate, broader, reopened, or reversed meaning. This is normative atomic consistency, not a database transaction or runtime atomicity claim. No transaction owner, row/table boundary, storage operation, lock, isolation level, algorithm, recovery, or delivery mechanism was selected.

### D01-OM-DD-13 — recovery, delivery, and ordering non-authority

Recovery, delivery, and transport order have no independent closure-meaning authority. Arrival, non-arrival, duplication, delay, recovery, or reordering cannot create, broaden, reopen, reverse, or prove the one effect. An occurrence reaching the normative decision boundary is evaluated under D01-OM-DD-01 through -12; repeated or reordered occurrences converge through re-evaluation. No exactly-once, at-least-once, delivery, ordering, recovery, queue/event/job, reliability, retry, execution, or persistence guarantee was selected.
