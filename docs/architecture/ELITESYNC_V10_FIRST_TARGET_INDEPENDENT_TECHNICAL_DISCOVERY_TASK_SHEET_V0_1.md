## 7. Demonstrably target-independent test

`STATIC / READ-ONLY != TARGET-INDEPENDENT`.

Every candidate question and every proposed finding must separately pass all tests below before it may be recorded as an observed target-independent static fact.

1. **Static observability:** Is the wording limited to what is directly visible in one or more allowlisted tracked artifacts at the authorized commit?
2. **Target removal:** If every concrete target remains unknown and the accepted-target count remains zero, does the claim remain true with identical wording, scope, and confidence?
3. **No embedded criterion:** Does the claim avoid depending on or implying latency, availability, consistency, recovery, capacity, cost, privacy, Safety, fairness, comprehension, accessibility, audit, retention, deletion/export, pacing, SLA/SLO, staffing, operational, quality, or configuration criteria?
4. **No alternative choice:** Does it avoid comparing, selecting, recommending, rejecting, optimizing, or preferring any architecture, schema, API, store, module/service, framework, runtime, infrastructure, deployment, model, or implementation alternative?
5. **No manufactured authority:** Does it avoid turning qualitative governance, a dependency declaration, default, 9.x history, vendor behavior, benchmark, competitor, sample, UI example, comment, unused code, or AI estimate into a target, requirement, accepted choice, or current behavior?
6. **No unresolved empirical dependency:** Does it remain true without U-05 participant/data activity, U-08 segment/readiness/desirability evidence, U-10 comprehension/accessibility validation, new Safety/legal authority, telemetry, measurement, or runtime evidence?
7. **No product or operations promise:** Does it avoid creating a user-facing promise, operating commitment, quality claim, acceptance criterion, or design-entry verdict?
8. **No conceptual-to-technical collapse:** Does it avoid treating Product Truth or a conceptual domain/information responsibility as a module, service, table, interface, owner, or deployment boundary?
9. **Exact provenance:** Can a reviewer reproduce the observation from an exact path, authorized commit, locator, and blob/hash without inspecting a prohibited source or executing project behavior?

Allowed results are:

- `PASS — DEMONSTRABLY TARGET-INDEPENDENT STATIC FACT`; or
- `FAIL / UNDEMONSTRATED — CLASSIFY ONLY; DO NOT INVESTIGATE OR DECIDE FURTHER`.

Any `No`, ambiguity, missing provenance, or required extra source produces the second result for the affected item. It must then be classified as `TARGET-DEPENDENT — BLOCKED`, `UNKNOWN — NOT ESTABLISHED`, `OUT OF SCOPE — NOT INSPECTED`, or `CONFLICTING EVIDENCE — STOP FOR REVIEW`. No target, assumption, source expansion, test, or technical judgment may be introduced to make it pass.

The stop is affected-item or affected-scope only unless repository authority, source containment, sensitive information, or upstream conflict compromises the entire tranche. It creates no global account/person meaning.
