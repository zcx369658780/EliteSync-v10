## 1. Canonical Meaningful Conversation product meaning

Accepted bounded meaning:

`Meaningful Conversation` is a consent-based human interaction in which a participant can recognize that they learned or clarified something material about fit or difference, intent, boundary, expectation, uncertainty, communication need, what to clarify next, or whether further exploration deserves attention.

- Meaning is participant-relative and context-specific.
- Different participants may reasonably experience the same Conversation differently.
- One participant's reflection is not objective truth about the other person and does not establish the other's consent.
- Meaningful does not mean positive, romantic, compatible, successful, long, emotionally intense, or destined to continue.
- A participant may optionally describe respectful clarification leading to pause or closure as meaningful; the product must not impose that label.
- The platform does not own, infer, or adjudicate the human meaning of a Conversation.

### 1.1 Controlling optional-label caveat

`MEANINGFUL CONVERSATION != PRODUCT-IMPOSED POSITIVE LABEL`.

`Meaningful Conversation` remains an optional participant-relative description. A harmful, unwanted, coercive, distressing, Safety-interrupted, boundary-violating, or otherwise negatively experienced interaction must not be reframed by the product as meaningful merely because a participant learned something or reached a clearer next decision.

A User may become more informed because an interaction clarified a boundary, prompted closure, or triggered protection without accepting the `Meaningful Conversation` label.

- `LEARNING / CLARIFICATION != PRODUCT-IMPOSED MEANINGFULNESS LABEL`.
- `LEARNING / A CLEARER NEXT DECISION != POSITIVE, WANTED, SAFE, HEALTHY, OR NON-COERCIVE EXPERIENCE`.
- `BECOMING MORE INFORMED != CONSENT, MUTUALITY, CONTINUED ACCESS, OR RELATIONSHIP PROGRESS`.
- `SAFETY / CLOSURE INFORMATION != ORDINARY CONVERSATION-QUALITY, PERSON, COMPATIBILITY, GUILT, OR SUCCESS MEANING`.

## 2. Canonical Informed Progression product meaning

`Informed Progression` means improved human decision quality under uncertainty. A User may become better informed about whether to continue exploring, clarify something, pause, close, or remain uncertain.

- It is not a platform-owned relationship-progress state.
- It does not define U-13 relationship-state vocabulary or create a state machine.
- Continuation is not better than closure, pause, or retained uncertainty.
- Activity does not establish Relationship state.
- One User's reported learning does not establish mutuality or another User's position.
- Boundary clarification, closure, or protection may better inform a User without imposing a Meaningful Conversation label or creating consent, mutuality, Relationship, person, or Safety truth.

## 3. Platform non-adjudication rule

None of the following automatically proves that a Conversation is meaningful or successful: message count, duration, response latency, response frequency, engagement, session time, sentiment score, emotional intensity, topic count, AI-generated summary, model confidence, inferred attraction, inferred intent, inferred psychological state, continued contact alone, or Match/Connection persistence alone.

Where separately authorized for another purpose, these may prove only bounded observed events. They do not establish human meaning, consent, Compatibility, Relationship state, success, or person value. Operational or telemetry labeling creates no evidence or authority.

## 4. Evidence and epistemic grammar

- `User Declared`: what a User says they learned, felt, or decided; authoritative only as that declaration.
- `Product Observed Event`: the bounded event/context only.
- `Derived Interpretation`: tentative and unable to upgrade source evidence.
- `AI-Generated Interpretation / Explanation`: supporting-only and unable to determine truth or private thought.
- `Unknown / Insufficient Evidence`: remains unknown and creates no adverse meaning.
- `Compatibility Hypothesis`: revisable only within separately accepted purpose/evidence rules.

`EPISTEMIC CLASS NAME != DEFAULT USER-FACING TRUTH LABEL`.

No Meaningful Conversation Score, new truth class, reviewed-person finding, relationship-success label, or evidence-strength promotion is authorized.

## 5. Privacy-safe measurement boundary

Current authority does not permit default use of private Conversation content, semantic extraction, embeddings, private-message features, sentiment/emotion inference, private-interaction-derived person traits, generalized model learning, or hidden private-message analytics.

Any future measurement proposal requires separately accepted purpose/data authority and cannot bypass U-14. Separate, material, or outcome-affecting private/shared interaction analytics/model use remains excluded from current MVP regardless of a secondary or supporting label. Ordinary operational telemetry remains unauthorized until separately reviewed and does not become meaningfulness evidence merely by being operational.

## 6. User reflection and Relationship Feedback

A future user-controlled reflection may record what the same User says they learned, what remains unknown, or whether the experience helped their next decision.

- It remains `User Declared` and purpose-classified.
- It is not truth about the other participant, a public review/rating, reputation, or desirability.
- It does not automatically enter Ranking or general model learning.
- No current collection, processing, Feedback-learning, or presentation authority is created by defining this semantic possibility.

## 7. Participant disagreement and asymmetry

- Preserve attributed positions; do not average them into person truth or force symmetry.
- Do not reveal one person's private reflection to the other by default.
- One person's meaningful experience does not imply the other's consent to continue.
- One person's not-meaningful experience does not become an adverse score on the other person.

## 8. Missing, no-response, and no-reflection handling

- No reflection means unknown, not negative evidence.
- No response is ambiguous, not proof of incompatibility or misconduct.
- Silence must not become low quality, low value, deception, rejection intent, or person defect.
- Lower evidence may only narrow, omit, or block the affected claim/use.

## 9. Safety boundary

- Safety reports, blocks, allegations, findings, case state, counts, and reasoning do not become ordinary Meaningful-Conversation quality metrics or Compatibility/Ranking/reputation/person signals.
- A Conversation ending for Safety reasons creates no ordinary person-quality or Compatibility meaning.
- Immediate protection may override ordinary continuation but does not establish guilt or Relationship truth.
- Ordinary contexts may receive only minimum-necessary enforcement directives, not Safety evidence or reasoning.
- No Safety Operations or Safety risk/prioritization authority is created.
- Safety or closure information may support the same User's next decision only within separately authorized semantics; it creates no ordinary Conversation-quality, person-quality, Compatibility, guilt, reputation, or success meaning.

## 10. Current MVP product-promise boundary

Accepted bounded product-policy promise: EliteSync may support Users in having more deliberate, explainable, consent-based conversations and reflecting on what they learned or still do not know, without imposing a positive Meaningful Conversation label on a harmful, unwanted, coercive, distressing, Safety-interrupted, or otherwise negative experience.

Current MVP/pre-alpha must not promise guaranteed meaningful conversations, objective Conversation-quality detection, private-message-based success prediction, automatic understanding of relationship progress, emotional/psychological truth detection, automatic relationship-success learning, hidden Conversation scoring, or private-content optimization/model learning.

This product-policy wording does not authorize a feature, measurement, telemetry, processing, or implementation.
