This candidate defines a technology-neutral implementation plan for Calm Home as a privacy-minimal, read-only composition over four independently authoritative inputs: Runtime Readiness, Canonical Match, Product Connection, and Conversation live-read/live-send evidence.

Home owns exactly zero lifecycle writers. It cannot author, correct, revoke, supersede, merge, rank, strengthen, repair, or execute a source-domain fact. ## 2. Controlling authority and common semantics

The controlling order is the current Owner task; accepted BA-06 Calm Home technical design; accepted IP-01 common contract; accepted IP-02 Runtime Readiness, IP-03 Canonical Match, IP-04 Product Connection, and IP-05 Messaging Consent plus Conversation live-gate plans; accepted post-BA01..07 dependency review; then this non-authoritative implementation plan. `Display-valid` means the source independently permits the minimum fact for the exact Home viewer/audience/purpose under its display freshness rule. `Action-guidance-valid` means the source's actor-relevant descriptive action evidence is authoritative, correctly bound, current, fresh, and admitted for Home candidacy. ## 4. Four source-adapter contracts

### 4.1 Runtime Readiness

The adapter binds authenticated subject, exact protected-use scope, Home audience/purpose, authority provenance, separate eligibility/checklist/verification/effective-readiness/presentation classes, prerequisite-set identity, dependency revisions, source-local revision/currentness, freshness, and condition. It may provide a coarse scope-specific effective-readiness label and a separately authorized actor-relevant next step. It never collapses the five classes, exposes another subject, infers launch eligibility, or uses raw verification/checklist evidence when a coarse authorized result suffices.

### 4.2 Canonical Match

The adapter binds authenticated viewer/role, exact participation context and current proposal identity where applicable, participant relation, Match audience/purpose, independent participation/proposal/decision lineages, currentness/freshness, and condition. It may provide minimum participant-authorized participation/proposal/outcome presentation and own-role descriptive action. It never exposes the other participant's private reason or raw decision detail, creates a total score, infers Product Connection, or treats one-sided acceptance as mutual outcome.

### 4.3 Product Connection

The adapter binds authenticated viewer, participant role, exact current Connection aggregate/request and participant set, Connection audience/Home purpose, current CN state, source-local revision/currentness, freshness, and condition. It may provide minimum current state and role-valid descriptive action. Historical/superseded aggregates remain explicit and non-actionable where separately display-authorized. Connection evidence never creates Messaging Consent, Conversation live access, or Relationship state.

### 4.4 Conversation live read/send

The adapter consumes only the accepted IP-05 privacy-minimal projection: separate live-read and live-send decisions, exact current Connection/Conversation-purpose/participant binding, evaluated independent CN and MC revision references, and coarse conditions. It requests no message, preview, draft, attachment, private unread/activity detail, or historical content. A live-read grant does not imply send; a live-send grant does not imply read or history. Any displayed navigation/control remains descriptive and the gate revalidates at the protected boundary.

## 5. Per-source admission and privacy-minimal envelope

For each intended display or action-guidance use, admission conjunctively validates:

1. accepted source owner and provenance;
2. exact source evidence class and domain identity/scope;
3. exact authenticated subject or complete participant binding;
4. exact Home audience and purpose;
5. actor and source-owned role for action guidance;
6. same-lineage controlling revision/currentness;
7. independent source/use-owned freshness;
8. correction/revocation/supersession relation;
9. source-local condition and known projection lag; and
10. privacy-minimal field eligibility for the intended Home section.

Any invalid, stale, superseded, unknown, unavailable, mismatched, incomparable, or lagged action evidence is excluded. A display may remain only when the source separately authorizes that precise coarse/historical display; it is explicitly non-actionable and cannot become primary.

The Home envelope makes only these meanings evaluable: section; source domain/class; minimum viewer/subject or participant binding; Home audience/purpose; source lineage and revision relation; separate currentness/freshness; minimum neutral label; source-local condition; separately authorized navigation; descriptive permitted action; and primary/non-primary presentation result. It creates no global lifecycle, global revision, cross-domain snapshot promise, hidden priority, or permission token.

## 6. Three-section projection

### 6.1 `Current state`

`Current state` contains a small privacy-minimal set of independently sourced facts. Each item remains source-faithful and independently bound; Home never fuses Readiness, Match, Connection and Conversation-grant vocabularies into one journey state. A state label does not imply a permitted action. Historical/coarse display is included only under separate source authority, marked non-current/non-actionable, and excluded from primary selection.

### 6.2 `Next decision`

`Next decision` contains only admitted actor-relevant source-authorized action guidance or separately authorized navigation to the owning decision surface. Every candidate carries source, actor/role, context, audience/purpose, source revision/currentness/freshness, minimum label, and revalidation requirement. Home does not submit, queue, reserve, or reconcile the mutation. No candidate may be synthesized from state labels, absence, transport success, routes, UI state, or another domain.

### 6.3 `Optional support`

`Optional support` is genuinely optional, quiet, non-blocking and non-urgent. An item requires an accepted source/category, Home audience/purpose, privacy-minimal label, and separately authorized destination where present. With no authorized catalog/item, the section is absent or empty. Empty space is not failure and is never filled with candidate-irrelevant Showcase, public-profile enrichment, private content, analytics, ranking, Safety, or unaccepted signals.

## 7. Deterministic at-most-one primary action

Primary selection is presentation filtering only:

1. build candidates only from current source-authorized actor-relevant action guidance;
2. exclude every candidate whose provenance, binding, currentness, freshness, audience/purpose, actor/role, or privacy-minimal display fails, or whose source is stale, superseded, unknown, unavailable, incomparable, mismatched, revoked, or lagged;
3. zero valid candidates yields no primary action;
4. exactly one valid candidate may be the sole primary action;
5. more than one valid candidate yields one primary only if a separately accepted, current, applicable Home precedence rule uniquely selects among those exact already-valid candidates;
6. without such uniquely applicable precedence, return no primary action; and
7. any primary remains descriptive and must be revalidated by the owning source at action time.

Home never breaks ties or ranks with cross-domain timestamps, revision numbers, arrival order, private Conversation content, unread/activity counts, countdowns, Compatibility totals/factors, hidden Safety evidence, model output, predicted engagement, inferred desirability, person worth, or unsupported urgency. A precedence rule, if later accepted, may filter valid candidates only; it cannot make an invalid candidate valid or alter a source outcome.

## 8. Partial availability and propagation

| Newer source correction/supersession | Displace only the affected old evidence and recompute its item/candidate plus primary selection. | Rewrite another domain or compare cross-domain revisions. |
| Known newer authority with projection lag | Suppress older current/action claim; show only permitted updating condition. | Roll back, fabricate details, or retain old action. |
| Incomparable same-source revisions | Mark/omit that source according to its privacy contract and deny its action guidance. | Last-received-wins or cross-domain tie-break. |

Home promises no synchronized cross-domain snapshot. It preserves each source revision vector and condition. A valid source cannot repair, refresh, replace, infer, or order another source.

## 9. Action and navigation revalidation

A Home action is a descriptive hint bound to the source identity, actor/role, subject/participants, aggregate/context, audience/purpose, and evaluated source revision/currentness/freshness. Before mutation or protected access, the owning source or gate revalidates those bindings and then-current authority. Stale Home cards, cached permitted actions, navigation targets, deep links, notifications, transport receipts, or client optimism never authorize.

Readiness actions return to Readiness authority; Match actions to Match authority; Connection actions to Connection authority; Conversation read/send to the separate IP-05 live evaluators and any applicable Conversation restriction. Home receives only the new authoritative result/projection later; it never reconciles or writes the outcome itself.

Readiness does not create Match; Match does not create Connection; Connection does not create Messaging Consent or Conversation grants; Conversation activity does not create Relationship. Home composition creates none of them.
