## 2. Durable vocabulary architecture

The following remain distinct authority/state classes:

1. introduction-supply participation;
2. Match opportunity lifecycle;
3. unilateral interest/pass declaration;
4. reciprocal Connection;
5. Conversation access;
6. ordinary closure/expiry;
7. Safety control state; and

No generic/global relationship-state concept may collapse them. A future technical representation or display label cannot erase provenance, authority, reciprocity, lifecycle, consent, visibility, correction, Safety separation, or human Relationship meaning.

## 3. Introduction-supply participation

`RECEIVING INTRODUCTIONS` means current User permission for future introduction supply for the named purpose. It does not mean Match Ready, eligible, candidate availability, supply priority, guaranteed supply, or person availability.

`INTRODUCTIONS PAUSED` means a User-controlled stop to future introduction supply. It is not Identity deletion, negative evidence, punishment, or person meaning.

`INTRODUCTIONS CLOSED` means future participation in introduction supply for that named purpose ended. It does not itself delete Identity/account, rewrite history, imply Relationship, imply rejection motive, imply wrongdoing, or imply Safety meaning.

## 4. Match opportunity semantics

`MATCH PRESENTED` means a bounded opportunity was presented only. It creates no mutuality, contact consent, Connection, Relationship, or Compatibility proof.

`INTEREST EXPRESSED` means unilateral `User Declared` interest only. It is not mutuality, contact consent, a promise, or another User's position.

`PASSED` means a neutral User choice. It creates no adverse person meaning.

`EXPIRED` means opportunity lifecycle cessation under a separately authorized timing policy. Expiry creates no motive, incompatibility, fault, rejection meaning, or person meaning.

No unilateral state creates Connection.

## 5. Connection and Conversation access

### 5.1 Connection

`CONNECTION` means reciprocal, current, revocable agreement to explore communication. It is not Relationship, friendship, commitment, exclusivity, permanence, off-platform consent, Compatibility proof, or consent to unrelated purposes.

`CONNECTION CLOSED` means affected future ordinary access under that Connection is no longer active under valid current lifecycle authority.

### 5.2 Conversation

`CONVERSATION OPEN` means bounded communication access exists under a valid current Connection and applicable current controls.

`CONVERSATION CLOSED` means that bounded communication access ended.

Neither open/closed state nor duration/activity creates emotional meaning, quality, success, failure, Compatibility, misconduct, Relationship, permanence, consent to private-content inspection, or consent to unrelated processing.

Do not create `Conversation Successful`, `Conversation Failed`, a Conversation Quality state, or an implied Relationship state.

### 5.3 Closure caveat and attributable authority

`CLOSED != MUTUAL AGREEMENT`.

`CONNECTION CLOSED` and `CONVERSATION CLOSED` mean only that affected ordinary future access is no longer active. They must not imply both participants mutually agreed to closure, either participant's motive, rejection, incompatibility, wrongdoing, guilt, person defect, Relationship failure, Safety finding, or Safety case reason.

Where material for lifecycle, rights, correction, revocation, review, reconstruction, appeal, or stale-effect cleanup, the attributable closure authority/reason class remains distinct. Distinct classes may include, where separately applicable, User choice, expiry, revocation, supersession, or a minimum-necessary Safety directive. These classes do not collapse into one human-meaning state. Restricted Safety evidence/reasoning must not be exposed through ordinary closure state.

`CONTROL DIRECTIVE != SAFETY EVIDENCE`.

- no response = ambiguous;
- pause = User control;

## 10. Lifecycle, correction, and revocation

Pause, reset, close, expire, correction, revocation, and supersession remain distinct.

A lifecycle change must remove or invalidate stale active dependent effects within the affected scope. Resumption requires current affirmative authority. Historical state, provenance, cache, availability, prior consent, or implementation state cannot recreate withdrawn active authority. Pause/close must not silently delete or rewrite another person's independent history or rights.

## 11. Safety and restriction separation

Ordinary vocabulary remains distinct from block, report, allegation, interim protection, reviewed restriction, appeal, reversal, and Safety case state.

Do not create a generic person-level `blocked`, `bad`, `unavailable`, or `unsafe` state that collapses distinct reasons.

A minimum-necessary Safety directive may stop only the affected supply, Match, Connection, or Conversation scope. It transfers no Safety evidence, allegation detail, guilt, reputation, Compatibility, desirability, Ranking, or person meaning.

## 12. Current product-promise boundary
