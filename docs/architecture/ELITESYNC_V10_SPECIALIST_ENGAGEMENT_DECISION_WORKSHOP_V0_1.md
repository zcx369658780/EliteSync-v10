## 2. Decision Package A — Reviewer Sourcing Authorization

### 2.1 Owner decision required

For each SRA separately, decide whether to authorize a named human sourcing lead to search for and identify potential reviewers through exact Owner-approved channels, within a candidate-count/time limit and package-specific qualification standard.

Required outcome record: `accepted | revised | rejected | deferred`, with allowed channels, systems, search terms, information fields, candidate-count/time bounds, verifier, custodian, and expiry. Approval does not authorize candidate evaluation, contact, selection, engagement, compensation, or review.

### 2.2 Scope and allowed activity

- Search only the exact authorized professional/institutional directories, verified affiliation pages, Owner-approved referrals, formal procurement panels, or direct Owner nominations.
- Identify potential reviewers only for the named SRA package.
- Record minimal public professional identity, role, qualification-source locator, apparent scope relevance, channel/date, and verification status.
- Produce a bounded sourcing log for later human verification and Owner review.

### 2.3 Forbidden activity

- Contacting, messaging, inviting, profiling beyond authorized professional facts, requesting references, selecting, appointing, hiring, or commissioning anyone.
- Automated outreach, scraped contact lists, data brokers, covert or sensitive inference, AI-generated identities, or unapproved general/social-media searches.
- Sharing package evidence, repository-private content, personal/sensitive/private-interaction/Safety/participant/case/production data, credentials, or confidential material.
- Promising work/payment, transferring a corpus, executing review, technical design, implementation planning, or code.

### 2.4 Responsible human role

An Owner-designated human sourcing lead performs the bounded search. A separate named human verifier checks identity/qualification-source provenance. The sourcing lead cannot self-authorize channels, contact candidates, accept conflicts, or appoint a reviewer. AI/tools may assist formatting only and cannot be identity evidence or authority.

### 2.5 Information and confidentiality boundary

Use only public professional information or Owner-approved non-confidential sourcing inputs necessary for the package. Candidate records are purpose-limited, access-restricted, provenance-linked, correctable, time-bounded, and retained/disposed by a named custodian. No project-confidential material is disclosed to a potential candidate during sourcing.

### 2.6 Spend boundary

`NONE`. Sourcing approval does not authorize subscription purchase, procurement fee, referral fee, compensation, reimbursement, gift, contract, or other commitment unless the same exact Owner record separately specifies approver, payee/provider, amount/cap, currency, purpose, and terms.

### 2.7 Stop conditions

Stop for missing/expired Owner authority; unapproved channel/system/search term; unclear candidate identity or source provenance; restricted/personal information need; package mismatch; conflict/independence concern requiring evaluation; candidate-specific contact; external disclosure; fee/commitment request; AI authority claim; or drift into review, design, or implementation.

## 3. Decision Package B — Candidate Evaluation Authorization

### 3.1 Owner decision required

For each SRA separately, decide whether a named human evaluation lead may assess already lawfully sourced candidates against an Owner-approved qualification, competence, conflict, independence, confidentiality, and package-fit standard. Evaluation authority cannot source new candidates, contact candidates/referees, select/appoint a reviewer, or authorize engagement.

### 3.2 Scope and allowed activity

- Review only the exact candidate records and public/Owner-approved qualification evidence named in the decision.
- Independently verify identity, qualification, competence scope, package fit, professional standing where relevant, conflicts, independence, limitations, expiry, and confidentiality capacity.
- Compare candidates only through the approved criteria and evidence; record `PASS | FAIL | NOT VERIFIED` per item.
- Produce a bounded candidate-evaluation record with options, rejection rationale where applicable, residual risks, and unresolved verification gaps for Owner decision.

### 3.3 Forbidden activity

- New sourcing/search, candidate or referee contact, interviews, negotiation, selection, appointment, work requests, compensation promises, or engagement.
- Inferring credentials, protected/sensitive traits, private information, conflicts, or authority; public reputation scoring; misconduct/guilt findings; or automated candidate ranking as decision authority.
- Corpus sharing, restricted/private data access, specialist review, legal/Safety findings, UNKNOWN disposition, technical design, implementation planning, or code.

### 3.4 Responsible human role

An Owner-designated human evaluation lead performs the assessment; an independent human verifier checks material identity/qualification/conflict evidence. A named custodian preserves the evaluation record. The Owner alone decides candidate acceptance/rejection/defer/revision for possible later contact. Evaluators cannot appoint or engage.

### 3.5 Information and confidentiality boundary

Use only the authorized candidate record, public qualification evidence, and minimal non-confidential package requirements. Candidate records are restricted to named evaluators, verifier, custodian, and Owner; access, correction, retention, expiry, and disposal must be recorded. Do not use project evidence corpus, personal/sensitive/private/Safety/participant/case/production information, credentials, or unapproved confidential data.

### 3.6 Spend boundary

`NONE`. Evaluation authority does not authorize paid verification, background check, reference service, travel, reimbursement, compensation, procurement, or engagement unless separately and exactly authorized.

### 3.7 Stop conditions

Stop for incomplete/unverifiable identity or qualifications; evidence conflict or expiry; unresolved material conflict/independence risk; package mismatch; need for candidate/referee contact; restricted information; unclear evaluation criteria or custodian; implied selection/engagement; fee/request; or drift into review, design, or implementation.

## 4. Decision Package C — Contact Authorization

### 4.1 Owner decision required

For each exact evaluated candidate and SRA, decide whether a named human sender may make a bounded contact through a named channel for a named purpose. The record must identify exact recipient, approved message/questions, disclosures/attachments, contact count/time/expiry, response custody, and stop/escalation path.

### 4.2 Scope and allowed activity

- Send an Owner-approved message to the exact recipient/account.
- Confirm identity, availability, qualification evidence, competence fit, conflicts, independence, confidentiality capacity, and interest in the bounded `Specialist Review Record` model.
- Explain explicitly that no engagement, work request, evidence transfer, payment, specialist authority, or review execution is authorized.
- Preserve message/response provenance within the authorized retention boundary.

### 4.3 Forbidden activity

- Contacting substitutes, colleagues, referees, institutions, referrals, or additional candidates.
- Requesting work, findings, legal opinion, Safety decision, review, design, implementation, or production access.
- Negotiating, selecting, appointing, promising engagement/payment, signing terms, accepting an NDA, or creating any commitment without separate exact authority.
- Disclosing evidence corpus, repository-private/confidential information, credentials, or personal/sensitive/private-interaction/Safety/participant/case/production data.

### 4.4 Responsible human role

The Owner or an exact Owner-designated human contact coordinator sends the message. AI/tools cannot send, negotiate, represent authority, or communicate autonomously. A named custodian records the contact and response; a named human handles confidentiality/incident escalation.

### 4.5 Information and confidentiality boundary

Disclosure is limited to the minimum public or Owner-approved non-confidential conceptual package summary needed for the authorized purpose. Recipient confidentiality, channel security, retention, training use, logging, and onward-disclosure protection must not be assumed. Any NDA, restricted disclosure, or corpus access requires separate exact Owner authorization.

### 4.6 Spend boundary

`NONE`. Contact does not authorize fee discussion beyond recording an unsolicited request, negotiation, compensation, reimbursement, contract, gift, procurement, or commitment.

### 4.7 Stop conditions

Stop for unclear/expired sender, recipient, channel, message, purpose, disclosure, or retention authority; identity/qualification conflict; confidentiality uncertainty; request for restricted information, work, review, fee, contract, broader scope, production/technical action, or evidence transfer; material conflict; or any message that could imply engagement.

## 5. Decision Package D — Engagement Authorization

### 5.1 Owner decision required

For one named, verified, evaluated, and contacted human specialist and one exact SRA, decide whether to establish a bounded engagement. The exact Owner record must bind reviewer authority, questions, non-objectives, allowed/forbidden activity, approved corpus/access, confidentiality/security, deliverables, ownership, compensation, duration, termination, GPT L3 route, and Owner acceptance route.

Engagement authorization does not itself execute the review. A separate final execution-readiness confirmation and exact execution task remain required.

### 5.2 Scope and allowed activity

- Establish the written engagement terms for the named specialist and exact package.
- Assign specialist authorship, project custody, human provenance verification, access/retention/correction responsibility, GPT L3 review route, and Owner acceptance/disposition route.
- Approve only the bounded future deliverable: `Specialist Review Record` plus exact package-authorized evidence summary and UNKNOWN-impact analysis.
- Approve only the enumerated corpus and access rights expressly named in the decision.

### 5.3 Forbidden activity

- Starting review before final readiness/execution authorization; cross-package or scope expansion; unapproved corpus access/transfer; participant or data activity; personal/sensitive/private/Safety/case/production data; production access.
- Formal legal opinion or compliance claim unless separately required and authorized; case/person-level Safety work; methodology data/experiment/model/metric/signal execution or approval.
- Automatic UNKNOWN disposition, OD/ADR/Phase 11 change, technical design, implementation planning, code, migration, infrastructure, or deployment.

### 5.4 Responsible human role

The Owner is the engagement and final acceptance/disposition authority. A named human engagement administrator/custodian may execute only the administrative acts expressly delegated. A named human verifier confirms readiness and corpus provenance. The specialist authors findings within scope. GPT L3 is an independent review gate, not Owner or specialist authority. AI/tools remain preparation aids only.

### 5.5 Information and confidentiality boundary

Only the exact Owner-approved corpus and non-confidential/sanitized materials named in the engagement may be accessed. The record must define source ownership/license, least-privilege access, permitted copying/quotation/transfer, onward disclosure, storage, retention, correction, withdrawal, disposal, incident handling, and post-termination duties. Unknown authority or confidentiality requires stopping.

### 5.6 Spend boundary

Default `NONE`. If paid engagement is proposed, exact Owner authorization must state budget owner/approver, payee, currency, amount/cap, basis, invoice/tax/expense terms, milestones, acceptance/payment relationship, cancellation liability, and prohibited outcome-contingent incentives. No agent may negotiate, promise, incur, approve, or transmit payment beyond exact authority.

### 5.7 Duration, termination, and stop conditions

The record must specify start date, end/expiry, milestones, source refresh triggers, suspension authority, and post-expiry rule. Terminate/suspend for authority or qualification gap; conflict/independence change; scope drift; confidentiality/security/provenance failure; restricted-data need; unsupported conclusion; unauthorized contact/disclosure; forbidden activity; refusal to preserve limitations/corrections; or design/implementation entry.

Termination duties include stop-work acknowledgement, no further contact except authorized closeout, material return/deletion or authorized retention, confidentiality survival, incident reporting, record status/supersession, and only already authorized financial obligations.

## 6. Package-specific decision overlays

### 6.1 SRA-RM-01 — Research methodology

- **Human competence:** Research design, construct/measurement validity, evidence synthesis, source hierarchy, uncertainty, missingness, transfer/generalization, and claim-strength calibration.
- **Scope:** U-01, U-05, U-06, U-07, U-09, U-12, and U-14 methodology questions only.
- **Exclusions:** Participant research; dataset/user/private/Safety data analysis; statistical or experiment execution; model creation; metric/threshold/signal approval; privacy/legal or Safety decisions; technical design or implementation.

### 6.2 SRA-PL-01 — Privacy/legal

- **Human competence:** Privacy, information rights, and data governance for a later Owner-selected jurisdiction, population, entity, and purpose. Initial role is `Privacy/legal specialist`; licensed counsel is not required unless later formal legal opinion authority is explicitly established.
- **Scope:** U-01, U-02, U-03, and U-14 only.
- **Exclusions:** Selecting/inferencing jurisdiction; legal opinion requirement; compliance/legality/certification claim; lawful-basis assumption; personal/restricted data; production/legal commitment; technical design or implementation.

### 6.3 SRA-SM-01 — Safety/moderation

- **Human competence:** Online Safety/moderation policy, harm-aware conceptual scenarios, responsibility/escalation, human review, recourse, false-positive/false-negative, and capacity limits.
- **Scope:** U-03, U-04, U-11, and U-12 only; conceptual, responsibility-focused, hypothetical, sanitized, and non-person-level.
- **Exclusions:** Real person/case/report/allegation/emergency; guilt/reputation judgment; person-level assessment/risk scoring; moderation/restriction/emergency action; external routing; Safety/private data; autonomous AI authority; technical design or implementation.

Each decision must be package-specific. Shared UNKNOWN IDs do not merge scopes, reviewer authority, evidence, findings, or outcomes.
