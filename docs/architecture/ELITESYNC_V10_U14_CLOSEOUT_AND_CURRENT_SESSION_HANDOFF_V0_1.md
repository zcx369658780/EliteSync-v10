### 2.1 Accepted affected-purpose boundary

The current U-14 exclusion applies whenever private/shared human-interaction meaning is used beyond the original interaction purpose for a separate, material, or outcome-affecting purpose involving:

- analytics;
- model improvement;
- signal learning;
- Ranking learning;
- personalization learning;
- model training/evaluation; or
- general product optimization.

Purpose and inferred meaning control classification. Storage location, technical representation, and technology label do not.

### 2.2 Controlling purpose caveat

`PRIMARILY IS NOT A SAFE HARBOR`.

The exclusion continues to apply if the affected purpose is called secondary, incidental, supporting, auxiliary, derivative, non-primary, renamed, subordinated, or bundled with another purpose. Renaming or demoting the purpose does not bypass the exclusion.

### 2.3 Information and use boundary

Current MVP/pre-alpha excludes at minimum:

- private Conversation mining;
- private-message signal extraction;
- semantic summaries of private Conversation for affected analytics/model use;
- private-message embeddings;
- features, feature vectors, or model inputs derived from private Conversation;
- derived interpretations used for affected analytics/model purposes;
- Conversation-derived Ranking;
- Conversation-derived Compatibility learning;
- Conversation-derived general analytics;
- hidden personalization from private/shared interaction meaning;
- default model training/evaluation on private Conversation;
- generalized learning from private/shared interaction meaning;
- private-interaction-derived reputation;
- private-interaction-derived desirability;
- private-interaction-derived person, behavioral, or Compatibility signals;
- Relationship Feedback promoted into universal/shared person truth;
- Relationship Feedback promoted into general model learning; and
- Safety evidence, reasoning, counts, allegations, cases, findings, or case state used for ordinary analytics/model improvement.

### 2.4 Transformation invariance and metadata

Converting information into metadata, a summary, embedding, feature, derived interpretation, model input, or another technical representation does not shed provenance, purpose, privacy, shared-rights, lifecycle, fairness, epistemic, or cross-context constraints.

Message/interaction metadata is within the U-14 exclusion when used to infer relational meaning, psychological meaning, desirability, Compatibility, Safety meaning, or behavioral/person meaning. Calling a field `metadata`, `telemetry`, or another technical label is not an exemption.

### 2.5 Controlling telemetry caveat

`OUTSIDE U-14 EXCLUSION != AUTHORIZED`.

Ordinary non-content operational telemetry may sit outside this specific private-interaction exclusion only when it:

- does not inspect private content;
- does not infer private/shared interaction meaning; and
- does not create an incompatible new purpose.

Being outside U-14 creates no telemetry-field, purpose, logging, retention, analytics, processing, or pipeline authority.

Every future telemetry proposal requires its own separately accepted named purpose and applicable data authority, lifecycle/retention authority, privacy authority, and legal authority where applicable.

If the telemetry/private-interaction boundary cannot be established under an accepted concrete purpose/data review, the affected use remains blocked.

## 3. Feedback, Safety, product-promise, and data boundaries

### 3.1 Relationship Feedback

- Relationship Feedback remains purpose-classified and non-reputational.
- It does not become universal/shared person truth or general model-learning input.
- A future bounded same-User reflection/feedback purpose may only be considered under separate authority.
- U-14 grants no current Feedback processing or learning authority.

### 3.2 Safety

- Safety evidence, reasoning, counts, allegations, cases, findings, and case state remain isolated from ordinary analytics/model improvement.
- Minimum-necessary Safety control directives are enforcement meaning only, not ordinary evidence or signals.
- No guilt, person-truth, reputation, Compatibility, or Ranking meaning may be created by crossing this boundary.
- U-14 creates no Safety Operations or Safety prioritization/risk authority.

### 3.3 Product-promise consequence

Current MVP/pre-alpha must not promise:

- that the system learns from private conversations;
- private-message-driven Compatibility improvement;
- hidden intimate/private-interaction personalization;
- automatic relationship-success learning from Conversation;
- private-interaction-derived desirability or reputation scoring; or
- Safety-derived ordinary personalization.

### 3.4 No collection for later

`EXCLUDED NOW` must not become `COLLECT / RETAIN NOW FOR POSSIBLE FUTURE USE`.

U-14 authorizes no speculative collection, speculative retention, shadow datasets, semantic-summary stockpiles, embeddings, features, model-learning copies, logging, future-use stockpiling, or legacy-data reuse.

Historical availability, custody, storage, broad consent, technical access, legacy implementation, or prior use creates no future authority.
