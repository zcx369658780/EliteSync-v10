## 5. Owner decisions

### Q1 — Construct Status

Owner outcome: `ACCEPTED`

Before empirical validation, Compatibility, Compatibility Hypothesis, Match Readiness, Meaningful Conversation, and Informed Progression remain conceptual or hypothesis-level constructs.

They must not be represented as validated measurements, objective properties, proven predictors, relationship-success probabilities, or deterministic truths. Compatibility remains a bounded, revisable decision-support hypothesis, not an objective truth about two people.

### Q2 — Activity Metric Boundary

Owner outcome: `ACCEPTED`

Message count, response rate, session time, conversation duration, match acceptance, and continued product engagement may later be used as operational or product telemetry where otherwise authorized.

By default, they must not be interpreted as relationship quality, relationship success, meaningful conversation, healthy progression, or compatibility validity.

`Engagement is not relationship success.`

Any stronger interpretation requires separate evidence.

### Q3 — Self-Report Evidence Rule

Owner outcome: `ACCEPTED`

`User declaration is authoritative as declaration, not as objective fact or future-behavior truth.`

A statement such as “I prefer slow relationship progression” establishes that the user currently declares that preference. It does not establish that the user will objectively behave that way in every future relationship.

Before any self-report item may enter future consequential Ranking, require:

- named purpose;
- item-level evidence review;
- fairness review appropriate to the use;
- valid consent and authority;
- user comprehension;
- a correction and update path;
- provenance preservation.

This confirms and sharpens existing ADR-003 and ADR-005 boundaries; it does not replace them.

### Q4 — AI Explanation Comprehension

Owner outcome: `ACCEPTED`

Before any real external testing or user-facing deployment involving AI-generated compatibility or reflection language, require at least one bounded user-comprehension check demonstrating that users can distinguish among:

- AI interpretation;
- user declaration;
- verified fact;
- unknown or uncertainty.

This does not block pre-alpha conceptual work, internal mockups, non-user prototype work, or AI-assisted research preparation. It becomes relevant before real user-facing exposure.
