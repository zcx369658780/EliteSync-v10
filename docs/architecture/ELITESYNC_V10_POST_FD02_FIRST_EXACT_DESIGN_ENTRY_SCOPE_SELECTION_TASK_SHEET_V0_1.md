For a candidate to pass, the workshop must answer `PASS` to every test:

1. **Eight-field exactness:** all Section 5 fields are singular, bounded, internally consistent, and non-placeholder.

3. **No design content:** the candidate can be stated without architecture, schema, endpoint, payload, store, module/service boundary, UI flow, state-management choice, infrastructure, or implementation mechanism.
4. **Product/domain/information precedence:** the candidate derives from accepted upstream authority rather than FD-02 names or bootstrap wording.
5. **FD-02 evidence discipline:** every invoked FD-02 fact maps to one of FD02-001 through FD02-018 with its exact limitation; no static declaration becomes a selected choice or runtime truth.
6. **UNKNOWN isolation:** every remaining UNKNOWN is mapped to the exact affected candidate claim and is either satisfied, honestly excluded, demonstrated independent, or blocking.
7. **No false exclusion:** exclusion removes a dependency only from the exact candidate; it does not resolve, waive, disprove, or globally neutralize the UNKNOWN.
8. **No target manufacture:** no qualitative governance label, static value, default, route, dependency, AI judgment, or convenience becomes a target or requirement.
9. **No empirical manufacture:** no segment, desirability, willingness, Match Readiness, comprehension, accessibility, fairness, Safety, privacy, reliability, or readiness success is inferred.
10. **No conceptual-to-technical collapse:** conceptual Product Truth/Domain/Information responsibilities are not turned into tables, endpoints, modules, services, screens, owners, or deployment units.
11. **Requirement-traceability readiness only:** the workshop evaluates prerequisites for later traceability; it does not create design requirements.
12. **Non-transitive authority:** candidate identification remains separate from design-entry eligibility, design authorization, design acceptance, and implementation authorization.

Any failed or undemonstrated test prevents disposition 1. The workshop may not weaken the candidate, invent an assumption, add a source, or select a different track merely to force a pass.
