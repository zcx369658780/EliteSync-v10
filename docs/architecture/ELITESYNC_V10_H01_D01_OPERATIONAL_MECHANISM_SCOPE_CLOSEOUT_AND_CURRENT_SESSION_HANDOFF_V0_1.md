## 6. Core semantic invariants

### 6.1 Person, Compatibility, and interaction-state meanings

- User is a person with agency, not a score, ranking, algorithmic representation, reputation value, or product verdict.
- A Compatibility Hypothesis is an explainable, revisable proposition for human exploration, not objective truth, Relationship truth, or a verdict about either person.
- `Compatibility Hypothesis != Match != Connection != Conversation != Relationship`.
- Match is a bounded opportunity for introduction, not Relationship truth, reciprocal consent, or permission for contact.
- Connection is a reciprocal, current, bounded, and revocable choice to permit communication.
- Conversation is a consent-bounded communication context; access to it is not Relationship.
- Relationship meaning and status remain human-defined and cannot be created automatically by platform activity.
- User declaration is not objective truth. AI interpretation is not verified fact. AI is support, not consent, guilt, Safety finding, or Relationship authority.

### 6.2 Consent, missingness, and adverse inference

- Profile creation is not matching consent; Match is not contact consent; only reciprocal current choice creates a Connection.
- Connection is not permanent consent and not Relationship status.
- Conversation participation is not consent to hidden inference, analytics, publication, model training, Ranking, or AI impersonation.
- Missing, unknown, opt-out, nonresponse, withdrawal, rejection, closure, block, report, or case state is not adverse person evidence.
- No such state may become guilt, fault, desirability, reputation, Compatibility, person worth, account truth, or global meaning.

### 6.3 Closure, block, report, and Safety separation

- `closure != block != report`.
- `CLOSED != MUTUAL AGREEMENT`.
- Closure stops future optional continuation under the exact Connection authority; it does not rewrite prior receipt or another person's independent authorship.
- Block and report remain separately governed first-class control/Safety paths and require no reciprocal agreement.
- Ordinary closure is neither Safety evidence nor a Safety finding.
- A minimum-necessary Safety control directive may cross into an affected ordinary scope only for enforcement.
- `CONTROL DIRECTIVE != SAFETY EVIDENCE`.
- Safety allegation, evidence, case-processing state, reviewed finding, and restriction remain distinct; there is no automatic allegation-to-evidence-to-finding chain.
- Safety evidence or hidden Safety reasoning must not enter ordinary Compatibility, Ranking, desirability, reputation, public scoring, or person-worth use.
- No closure, block, report, Safety directive, or Safety case state creates adverse person/account/Relationship/global meaning.
