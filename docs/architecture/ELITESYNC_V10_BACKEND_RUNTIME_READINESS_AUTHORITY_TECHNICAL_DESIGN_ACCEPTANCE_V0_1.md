- presentation state != Readiness authority;

## 3. Accepted effective-readiness derivation

Effective readiness is accepted as a scope-specific, recomputable authority conclusion for one exact protected feature/use.

`READY` may be derived only when:

1. the applicable prerequisite set has already been separately product-authorized for that exact scope;
2. every required prerequisite is independently established by its owning authority;
3. every required claim is bound to the authenticated subject and exact audience/purpose;
4. every required claim is the current controlling revision and satisfies its own usable-freshness rule; and
5. no required claim is unknown, unavailable, stale, superseded, incomparable, mismatched, unestablished, or explicitly non-satisfied.

If the prerequisite set itself is unknown or unestablished, `READY` cannot be derived. The derivation cannot infer an empty set and cannot add prerequisites.

A later correction, revocation, or superseding revision of any contributing prerequisite invalidates every dependent prior `READY` conclusion until the derivation is recomputed from current evidence.

## 4. Projection and fail-closed behavior

The authenticated-subject projection correctly preserves separate claim classes, source owner/provenance, claim scope, revision/currentness, freshness, correction/revocation effects, effective-readiness dependency evidence, and presentation/authority conditions.

Unknown, unavailable, stale, superseded, incomparable, subject-mismatched, audience-mismatched, purpose-mismatched, provenance-unestablished, or otherwise invalid evidence cannot unlock a protected use.

`UNKNOWN != ABSENT` and `STATE VOCABULARY != AUTHORITY` remain binding.

No client enum, local completion flag, route, cache, optimistic success, ready-looking UI, or transport acknowledgement can author or restore Runtime Readiness authority.
