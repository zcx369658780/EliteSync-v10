## 2. Durable Meaningful Conversation semantics

`Meaningful Conversation` is an OPTIONAL, participant-relative, context-specific description within a consent-based human interaction.

A participant may use that description when they recognize that they learned or clarified something material about:

- fit or difference;
- intent;
- boundary;
- expectation;
- uncertainty;
- communication need;
- what to clarify next; or
- whether further exploration deserves attention.

Hard boundaries:

- the platform does not own Conversation meaning;
- the platform does not infer or adjudicate meaningfulness;
- participants may experience the same Conversation differently;
- one participant's reflection is not truth about the other;
- meaningfulness does not establish consent or mutuality; and
- meaningful does not mean positive, romantic, compatible, successful, long, emotionally intense, or destined to continue.

### 2.1 Controlling optional-label caveat

`MEANINGFUL CONVERSATION != PRODUCT-IMPOSED POSITIVE LABEL`.

A harmful, unwanted, coercive, distressing, boundary-violating, Safety-interrupted, or otherwise negatively experienced interaction MUST NOT be reframed by the product as meaningful merely because a User learned something or reached a clearer next decision.

A User may become more informed because an interaction clarified a boundary, prompted pause or closure, triggered protection, or clarified uncertainty, without accepting the `Meaningful Conversation` label.

- `LEARNING / CLARIFICATION != PRODUCT-IMPOSED MEANINGFULNESS LABEL`.
- `LEARNING / CLEARER DECISION != POSITIVE EXPERIENCE`.
- `LEARNING != CONVERSATION QUALITY`.
- `LEARNING != COMPATIBILITY`.
- `LEARNING != SUCCESS`.
- `BECOMING MORE INFORMED != CONSENT`.
- `BECOMING MORE INFORMED != MUTUALITY`.
- `BECOMING MORE INFORMED != RELATIONSHIP PROGRESS`.

## 3. Durable Informed Progression semantics

`Informed Progression` means improved human decision quality under uncertainty.

A User may become better informed about whether to:

- continue exploring;
- clarify something;
- pause;
- close; or
- remain uncertain.

It is not a platform-owned Relationship-progress state, a U-13 relationship-state decision, a state machine, continuation preference, inferred Relationship status, mutuality, consent, continued-access authority, or proof of success. Continuation is not automatically superior to pause, closure, or uncertainty.

## 4. Platform non-adjudication and epistemic force

None of the following automatically proves meaningfulness or success:

- message count;
- Conversation duration;
- response latency or frequency;
- engagement or session time;
- sentiment, emotion inference, or emotional intensity;
- topic count;
- AI summary or model confidence;
- inferred attraction, intent, or psychological state;
- continued contact; or
- Match or Connection persistence.

Where separately authorized for another purpose, these prove only bounded observed events. They do not establish human meaning, consent, Compatibility, Relationship state, success, or person value.

U-07 epistemic force remains controlling:

- `User Declared` = declaration only;
- `Product Observed Event` = bounded event/context only;
- `Derived Interpretation` cannot upgrade source evidence;
- `AI-Generated Interpretation / Explanation` is supporting-only;
- `Unknown / Insufficient Evidence` has no adverse meaning; and
- `Compatibility Hypothesis` remains separately purpose/evidence-gated.

`EPISTEMIC CLASS NAME != DEFAULT USER-FACING TRUTH LABEL`.

U-09 creates no Meaningful Conversation Score, Conversation-quality score, new truth class, reviewed-person finding, relationship-success label, or evidence-strength promotion.

## 5. Privacy, U-14, and telemetry boundary

`PRIMARILY IS NOT A SAFE HARBOR`.

## 6. Relationship Feedback, disagreement, and missingness

### 6.1 Relationship Feedback and reflection

A future same-User reflection may conceptually describe what that User says they learned, what remains unknown, or whether the experience helped their next decision.

It remains `User Declared`, purpose-classified, and non-reputational. It is not truth about another participant, a public review, reputation, desirability, automatic Ranking input, or general model-learning input.

U-09 creates no current Feedback collection, Feedback processing, Feedback-learning, or presentation authority.

### 6.2 Disagreement and asymmetry

- preserve attributed positions;
- do not average positions into person truth;
- do not force symmetry or infer mutuality;
- do not reveal one person's private reflection to another by default;
- one person's meaningfulness does not imply the other's consent; and
- one person's not-meaningful reflection creates no adverse score.

### 6.3 Missingness

No reflection is `Unknown`. No response is ambiguous. Silence does not establish low Conversation quality, low human value, deception, rejection intent, incompatibility, misconduct, or person defect. Lower evidence may only narrow, omit, or block the affected claim or use.

## 7. Safety boundary

Safety reports, blocks, allegations, findings, case state, counts, and reasoning do not become Conversation-quality metrics, Meaningful Conversation metrics, Compatibility, Ranking, reputation, desirability, person-quality signals, or success signals.

A Conversation ending for Safety reasons creates no ordinary person-quality, Compatibility, guilt, reputation, or success meaning. Immediate protection may override continuation without establishing guilt or Relationship truth. Ordinary contexts may receive only minimum-necessary Safety control directives.

U-09 creates no Safety Operations or Safety risk/prioritization authority.

## 8. Current product-promise boundary

Current product policy may say EliteSync supports more deliberate, explainable, consent-based conversations and reflection on what Users learned or still do not know.

Current MVP/pre-alpha must not promise:

- guaranteed meaningful conversations;
- objective Conversation-quality detection;
- private-message success prediction;
- automatic Relationship-progress understanding;
- emotional or psychological truth detection;
- automatic relationship-success learning;
- hidden Conversation scoring;
- private-content optimization; or
- private-content model learning.
