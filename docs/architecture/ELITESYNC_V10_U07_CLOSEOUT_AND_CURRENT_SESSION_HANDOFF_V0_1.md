### 2.1 Eight non-collapsing epistemic classes

The following classes are non-collapsing source/force governance semantics, not a numeric truth hierarchy:

1. `User Declared`;
2. `Product Observed Event`;
3. `Externally Supported / Verified Fact`;
4. `Derived Interpretation`;
5. `AI-Generated Interpretation / Explanation`;
6. `Compatibility Hypothesis`;
7. `Reviewed Finding`; and
8. `Unknown / Insufficient Evidence`.

Structured form, repetition, storage, historical use, AI generation, availability, popularity, or engagement cannot promote evidence strength.

- `User Declared` is authoritative only as a declaration. It is not an objective trait, honesty proof, stable identity, or future conduct. Self-report remains contextual, time-sensitive, correctable, and revocable. Missing declaration creates no adverse inference.
- `Product Observed Event` proves only the bounded event and context. It does not establish motive, private thought, stable trait, attraction, worth, intent, future conduct, causal meaning, or Compatibility truth. Exposure, engagement, popularity, response latency, rejection, Match/message scarcity, and product-created opportunity are not neutral ground truth.
- `Derived Interpretation` cannot upgrade the strength of its source evidence.
- `AI-Generated Interpretation / Explanation` is supporting-only. AI may not upgrade evidence; create consent, purpose, lifecycle, or consumer authority; infer private thoughts; create diagnosis, destiny, guilt, Connection, Relationship state, Safety finding, or a new Ranking signal. Model confidence and fluent wording are not evidence strength.
- `Compatibility Hypothesis` remains pair-specific, dimension-specific, uncertain, explainable, correctable, rejectable, and revisable. It is not scalar truth, probability of relationship success, guaranteed outcome, Safety clearance, diagnosis, destiny, or worth.
- `Unknown / Insufficient Evidence` may lead only to omission, lower confidence, uncertainty disclosure, narrower purpose, affected-use block, review routing, or preservation of attributed positions. It does not become negative person meaning, deception, guilt, risk, incompatibility, or permission to infer, collect, or process more.

### 2.2 Controlling epistemic-class caveat

`EPISTEMIC CLASS NAME != DEFAULT USER-FACING TRUTH LABEL`.

Epistemic class names are governance semantics first. They do not themselves strengthen evidence or create new or independent authority.

For `Externally Supported / Verified Fact`:

- `Verified` applies only to the exact claim actually supported by separately authorized evidence and verification;
- it remains bounded to accepted source, purpose, time, and scope;
- it does not mean the User as a whole is verified, verified character, stable trait, future conduct, unrelated-context truth, or perpetual truth;
- it remains correctable, expirable, and supersedable; and
- it creates no unrelated-purpose or cross-context authority.

For `Reviewed Finding`:

- it remains bounded to a separately authorized review purpose and scope;
- it creates no Owner acceptance beyond an explicit recorded Owner decision;
- it creates no Ranking, ordinary-domain, cross-context, or Safety authority outside separately accepted authority; and
- it does not become universal person truth.

### 2.3 Signal provenance and authority contract

Any future candidate signal must preserve at minimum:

1. source/provenance;
2. author/observer class where applicable;
3. named purpose;
4. epistemic status;
5. time relevance/freshness;
6. uncertainty/evidence strength;
7. sensitivity/privacy class where applicable;
8. lifecycle authority/status;
9. permitted consumer/use class;
10. correction/revocation behavior; and
11. dependent-output invalidation behavior.

Custody, storage, visibility, availability, prior use, analytics access, historical correlation, or implementation convenience creates no evidence, purpose, lifecycle, or consumer authority.

### 2.4 Evidence-to-claim proportionality and candidate eligibility

Claim force and wording may never exceed the strongest accepted evidence. Unsupported causal, predictive, diagnostic, deterministic, moral-character, private-thought, guilt, destiny, or relationship-success claims remain prohibited.

Every future signal class or item must pass the accepted eleven-part eligibility gate:

1. named purpose and direct relevance;
2. source/provenance clarity;
3. epistemic-status and evidence-strength classification;
4. user comprehensibility appropriate to the claim;
5. correction and revocation support;
6. lifecycle authority;
7. privacy/sensitivity review where applicable;
8. fairness/proxy/missingness review where applicable;
9. no accepted prohibition;
10. bounded explanation semantics; and
11. stop/review behavior for missing, stale, conflicting, or insufficient evidence.

Passing this checklist does not validate the item, place it on a field-level allowlist, authorize collection, or authorize processing.

## 4. Use, correction, and expansion boundaries

### 4.1 Use-class non-inheritance

Ranking/Matching presentation, Explanation, Optional Reflection, User Control/pacing, Relationship Feedback learning, and Safety processing remain separate non-inheriting use classes.

- Explanation eligibility does not create Ranking eligibility.
- Reflection eligibility does not create Ranking eligibility.
- Control eligibility does not establish a person trait.
- Feedback-learning eligibility does not establish reputation or universal desirability.
- Safety authority does not create ordinary signal authority.

### 4.2 Correction, revocation, and invalidation

Every correction, revocation, reversal, or supersession requires:

1. authoritative source change;
2. stale dependent active-effect cleanup, invalidation, or withdrawal; and
3. separate renewed-use or recomputation authority.

Provenance, history, caches, summaries, derived interpretations, or AI output must not conceptually preserve or recreate withdrawn active authority.

### 4.3 New-purpose and expansion gate

Every new signal, concrete item, consumer, use class, or purpose must stop for separate review unless already covered by accepted authority. A proposal must preserve its purpose, item/class, provenance, epistemic status, claim strength, privacy/sensitivity, fairness/proxy/missingness, comprehension/control, lifecycle/correction, affected population, stop/remedy, and dependency class.

Analytics, storage, model, historical, or implementation convenience creates no new signal or purpose authority.
