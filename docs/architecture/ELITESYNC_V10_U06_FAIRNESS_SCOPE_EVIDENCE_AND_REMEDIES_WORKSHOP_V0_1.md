## 3. Fairness purpose and scope model

### Observed / Accepted Authority

Fairness is already a purpose-specific product obligation constrained by Privacy, Safety, User Control, provenance, and human authority.

### Prior Conceptual Exploration

QA-FAIR-01 centered a proposed signal expansion in Compatibility/Matching and warned against a universal score.

### Advisory Input

Track A cautions that a construct, population, comparison, and claim strength must be defined before measurement.

### Product Inference

Different purposes allocate different benefits, burdens, errors, exposure, and recourse; equal romantic outcomes are neither controllable nor an appropriate fairness target.

### Owner-Accepted Product Decision

Define fairness as purpose-specific governance of unjustified material differential burden, exclusion, exposure, error, or recourse. A separate fairness scope is required for:

1. Compatibility signal and hypothesis use;
2. Matching eligibility, presentation, pacing, and opportunity allocation;
3. explanation and uncertainty presentation;
4. control, correction, contest, and lifecycle effects;
5. Safety restriction/review process where fairness applies without weakening immediate protection or containment;
6. analytics, evaluation, or experimentation proposals; and
7. any new purpose plausibly creating material differential effects.

Fairness does not mean equal romantic outcomes, guaranteed Matches, equal personal preferences, forced reciprocal access, override of consent/boundaries, one person-worth score, or equal treatment where relevant purposes and authority legitimately differ.

Dependencies: `PRODUCT RULE CAN BE OWNER-DECIDED NOW`; application requires `REQUIRES LATER QUALIFIED FAIRNESS / RESEARCH-METHOD REVIEW`; jurisdiction-specific obligations `REQUIRES LATER LEGAL AUTHORITY`.

## 4. Harm taxonomy

### Observed / Accepted Authority

Human-worth Ranking, unsupported inference, Safety leakage, stale adverse residue, and denial of correction/control are already prohibited or constrained.

### Prior Conceptual Exploration

QA-FAIR-01 referred generally to unequal behavior and harm distribution without accepting a taxonomy.

### Advisory Input

Track A recommends naming the estimand/construct and plausible alternative explanations rather than treating any difference as one harm.

### Product Inference

A later evaluation needs a closed harm hypothesis per purpose, but not every harm applies to every purpose.

### Owner-Accepted Product Decision

Use this non-universal product-policy taxonomy:

| Harm class | Product-policy meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Opportunity/exposure disadvantage | Unjustified reduction, concentration, or burden in an opportunity/exposure governed by the product |
| Function exclusion/denial | Unjustified loss or impairment of product function, access, control, or recourse |
| Differential error burden | Unequal material burden from unsupported, stale, false-positive, false-negative, or low-evidence inference |
| Explanation/recourse disparity | Materially weaker explanation, uncertainty disclosure, correction, contest, or remedy for an affected population/person |
| Proxy/sensitive-trait harm | Adverse effect arising from direct, inferred, or proxy treatment without accepted necessity and authority |
| Missingness/opt-out penalty | Adverse person meaning or product burden created merely by missing, sparse, refused, or withdrawn optional information |
| Feedback-loop amplification | Prior product exposure or response recursively increases later opportunity/burden without independent justification |
| Stereotype/representation harm | Product language or categorization reinforces unsupported group or person generalizations |
| Autonomy/consent harm | Product allocation or pressure undermines bounded choice, refusal, revocation, or another person's boundary |
| Privacy burden | Fairness activity or product use imposes unjustified collection, inference, disclosure, retention, or review exposure |
| Stale/corrected-data residue | Adverse active effect remains after authoritative correction, revocation, reversal, or supersession |
| Safety-process harm | Unequal process, explanation, contest, or restriction burden without converting allegation/case state into person truth or weakening protection |

Dependencies: taxonomy adoption is `PRODUCT RULE CAN BE OWNER-DECIDED NOW`; applicability and evidence are `REQUIRES LATER QUALIFIED FAIRNESS / RESEARCH-METHOD REVIEW`; Safety process targets `DEPENDS ON U-12 QUALITY / TARGET AUTHORITY`.

### Owner-Accepted Product Decision

| --- | --- | --- |
| Conceptual fairness risk/hypothesis | Justifies policy discussion or later review question | Not an observed harm/finding |
| Documented product behavior | Establishes what accepted documentation or an authorized observation shows | Not a population effect or fairness conclusion |

## 10. Stop, hold, narrow, rollback, and expansion rules

### Observed / Accepted Authority

Unknown authority creates no permission within the affected scope. Fail-closed must not become global person restriction or character meaning.

### Prior Conceptual Exploration

QA-FAIR-01 proposed preventing expansion under unknown/insufficient evidence.

### Advisory Input

Track A recommends predeclared stop conditions and narrower claims when evidence is weak.

### Product Inference

Policy can define categorical gates without inventing numeric thresholds.

### Owner-Accepted Product Decision

For the specific affected purpose/use:

- **block before use** when purpose/harm is undefined, data authority absent, an accepted prohibition would be violated, or no remedy/contest path exists for a consequential use;
- **hold for review** when affected population cannot reasonably be evaluated, proxy/sensitive risk is unresolved, required U-07/U-08/U-12/legal facts are absent, or evidence is contradictory;
- **narrow** population, signal, claim, exposure, or purpose when only a smaller scope is supportable and authorized;
- **rollback/withdraw** the affected use when later evidence, correction, revocation, or review invalidates its authority or accepted basis;
- **do not expand** while missingness creates unexplained adverse burden or evidence is too weak for the proposed claim.

The safe result is bounded denial, omission, cessation, or removal of the affected promise—not person-level guilt, risk, defect, worth, or global restriction.

Dependencies: semantics are `PRODUCT RULE CAN BE OWNER-DECIDED NOW`; applied triggers require `REQUIRES LATER QUALIFIED FAIRNESS / RESEARCH-METHOD REVIEW`, `DEPENDS ON U-07 SIGNAL / EVIDENCE STANDARD`, `DEPENDS ON U-08 SEGMENT / READINESS EVIDENCE`, and `DEPENDS ON U-12 QUALITY / TARGET AUTHORITY`; prohibited uses are `PROHIBITED BY ACCEPTED AUTHORITY`.
