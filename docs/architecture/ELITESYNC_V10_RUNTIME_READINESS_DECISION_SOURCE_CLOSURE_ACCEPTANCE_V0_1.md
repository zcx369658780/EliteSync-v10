## 2. Accepted effective-readiness rule

Effective readiness is a derived authority conclusion for one exact protected feature/use scope. It is not an independent user-editable state, a presentation label, or a universal readiness flag.

`READY` may be derived only when:

1. the applicable prerequisite set and every prerequisite class in it have already been product-authorized for that exact scope;
2. every required prerequisite is independently established by its owning authority;
3. each required claim is bound to the authenticated subject and the exact audience and purpose of the protected use;
4. each required claim is the current controlling revision in its own lineage and satisfies its usable-freshness rule; and
5. no required claim is unknown, unavailable, stale, superseded, incomparable, mismatched, not established, or explicitly non-satisfied.

If the applicable prerequisite set itself is unknown or unestablished, `READY` cannot be derived.

The rule cannot add a prerequisite. Prerequisite membership requires separate product authority.

## 3. Accepted fact-class separation

The following remain distinct:

- eligibility;
- required checklist completion;
- verification state for an exact verified fact/scope;
- effective readiness as a recomputable derived conclusion; and
- presentation state.

Accepted non-implications include:

- checklist completion is not verification, eligibility, launch readiness, or effective readiness by itself;
- verification is not global identity truth, minimum identity assurance, eligibility, launch readiness, or effective readiness by itself;
- eligibility is not verification, checklist completion, launch eligibility, or universal readiness;
- presentation state is not Readiness authority;
- effective readiness for one feature/use does not imply readiness for another scope or production launch readiness.

## 4. Revision, correction, revocation, and fail-closed behavior

Every contributing prerequisite retains its own authority owner, provenance, subject, audience, purpose, lineage/revision, currentness, and usable freshness.

A newer authority-authored correction, revocation, or superseding revision of any prerequisite invalidates every prior derived `READY` conclusion depending on that affected claim/scope. Cached or projected older evidence cannot remain an unlock token.

Unknown, unavailable, stale, superseded, incomparable, provenance-unestablished, subject-mismatched, audience-mismatched, purpose-mismatched, or otherwise unestablished evidence prevents `READY` and prevents the protected unlock.

`UNKNOWN != ABSENT`, `STATE VOCABULARY != AUTHORITY`, and `TRANSPORT SUCCESS != AUTHORITATIVE DOMAIN OUTCOME` remain binding.
