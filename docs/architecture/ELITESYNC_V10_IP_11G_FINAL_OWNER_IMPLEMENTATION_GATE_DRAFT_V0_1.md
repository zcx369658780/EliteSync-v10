## 4. Exact allowed implementation semantics

The source file may contain only dependency-free, deterministic, in-memory contract representation and pure validation/classification behavior required to prove the following semantics:

1. Evidence is bound to one explicit authority owner and authority scope.
2. Evidence is bound to the actor and actor role relevant to the attempted use.
3. Evidence is bound to the exact subject or complete participant set, as applicable.
4. Evidence is bound to the exact audience and purpose/context of use.
5. Aggregate/context identity is explicit and cannot be substituted by adjacency, route, cache, delivery or legacy identity.
6. Source condition distinguishes at least `PRESENT`, `ABSENT`, `UNKNOWN`, `UNAVAILABLE`, `STALE`, `SUPERSEDED` and `INCOMPARABLE`; `UNKNOWN` must never collapse to `ABSENT`.
7. Revision is source-local and meaningful only inside the same authority, scope, lineage and aggregate/context binding.
8. Cross-lineage revisions are incomparable; the contract must expose no global revision and no last-received-wins ordering.
9. Currentness states whether evidence is the controlling source-local revision; freshness separately states whether that current evidence is usable for the bound purpose.
10. Currentness does not imply freshness, and freshness must not manufacture currentness.
11. One logical-intent identity is permanently bound to one immutable set of semantic inputs.
12. An exact duplicate is recognizable only when the logical-intent identity and every semantic input are identical.
13. Reuse of an intent identity with any changed semantic input is rejected as changed-input/ambiguous reuse.
14. Idempotency identity is correlation only; it grants no permission and proves neither domain success nor transport success.
15. Authoritative outcome classification distinguishes committed, rejected and unknown.
16. Transport ambiguity after possible submission maps to unknown authoritative outcome until source-authoritative reconciliation establishes otherwise.
17. Transport observation alone cannot turn an unknown outcome into committed or rejected.
18. Correction, revocation or supersession relation invalidates displaced evidence and its privacy-minimal derived projection.
19. Invalidation does not reset a lifecycle, reopen a terminal identity, create a new aggregate or synthesize a source-domain transition.
20. Privacy-minimal projection exposes only bounded opaque synthetic references and semantic condition/outcome categories needed to explain the contract result.
21. Projection is not source authority, a bearer capability, permission, success proof or substitute writer.
22. Every protected evaluation fails closed when a required binding is missing, mismatched, unknown, unavailable, stale, superseded or incomparable.

The implementation may choose private internal organization inside the single source file only. It must not create a reusable cross-project framework, domain lifecycle, product policy, transport abstraction, persistence adapter or production integration.
