## 1. Affected purpose and seven information classes

The affected purpose is any current-MVP/pre-alpha use of private or shared human-interaction meaning for a separate, material, or outcome-affecting purpose involving analytics, model improvement, signal learning, Ranking learning, personalization learning, model training/evaluation, or general product optimization beyond the original interaction purpose.

Purpose and inferred meaning, not storage location or technology name, control classification.

### 1.1 Controlling purpose caveat

`PRIMARILY IS NOT A SAFE HARBOR`.

The current U-14 exclusion applies whenever private/shared interaction meaning is used for a separate, material, or outcome-affecting purpose involving analytics, model improvement, signal learning, Ranking learning, personalization learning, model training/evaluation, or general product optimization. This remains true even if the use is described as secondary, incidental, supporting, auxiliary, derivative, or otherwise not the primary purpose. Renaming, subordinating, or bundling the purpose does not bypass the exclusion.

1. **Private Conversation message content.** Current authority excludes mining or inspecting it for the affected purpose.
2. **Semantic summaries, embeddings, and derived interpretations of private Conversation.** Transformation does not shed source provenance, purpose, privacy, shared-rights, lifecycle, or epistemic limits.
3. **Message/interaction metadata used to infer relational, psychological, desirability, Compatibility, Safety, or behavioral meaning.** Such meaning-inference use falls inside the affected purpose; a `metadata` or `telemetry` label is not an exemption.
4. **Shared or mutually authored interaction information.** Authorship, visibility authority, subject-of-information interest, product custody for a named purpose, and lifecycle authority remain distinct. One participant, custody, storage, or prior visibility cannot grant all reuse rights.
5. **Relationship Feedback.** It remains purpose-classified and non-reputational. A future same-User reflection/feedback purpose may only be considered under separate authority; this workshop creates none.
6. **Safety-controlled information.** Evidence, reasoning, counts, allegations, cases, case state, and findings remain isolated from ordinary analytics/model improvement. A minimum-necessary control directive is enforcement meaning only, not evidence or a signal.
7. **Ordinary non-content operational telemetry.** Telemetry that does not inspect private content, infer private/shared interaction meaning, or create an incompatible new purpose is not silently swept into the U-14 exclusion. This classification creates no telemetry field, logging, retention, analytics, or processing authority. If the distinction cannot be made without concrete purpose/data review, the affected use stays blocked.

### 1.2 Controlling telemetry caveat

`OUTSIDE U-14 EXCLUSION != AUTHORIZED`.

Ordinary non-content operational telemetry may remain outside this specific private-interaction exclusion only when it does not inspect private content, infer private/shared interaction meaning, or create an incompatible new purpose. Being outside U-14 creates no telemetry field, purpose, logging, retention, analytics, or processing authority. Any future telemetry use requires its own separately accepted named purpose and applicable data, lifecycle/retention, privacy, and legal authority where applicable.

## 2. Owner-selected current-MVP exclusion direction

The Owner-selected direction removes from current MVP promises and design scope at minimum:

- private Conversation mining and private-message signal extraction;
- semantic summaries, embeddings, or derived interpretations of private Conversation for the affected purpose;
- Conversation-derived Ranking, Compatibility, personalization, or general analytics;
- private-message embeddings, features, feature vectors, or model inputs;
- default model training or evaluation on private Conversation;
- generalized learning from private or shared interaction meaning;
- private-interaction-derived reputation, desirability, person, behavioral, or Compatibility signals;
- Relationship Feedback promoted into universal/shared person truth or general model learning; and
- Safety evidence, reasoning, counts, allegations, cases, findings, or case state used for ordinary analytics/model improvement.

The exclusion would be a deliberate absence of the purpose, not merely a statement that implementation has not started. It would not establish that the purpose is universally unlawful, unsafe, impossible, permanently prohibited, or universally undesirable. Its basis is current product scope and the absence of accepted authority/evidence.

## 3. Product-promise and data/history consequences

Under the Owner-selected direction, current MVP language must not promise that:

- the system learns from private conversations;
- private messages improve Compatibility;
- hidden personalization uses intimate/private interaction;
- relationship success is automatically learned from conversations;
- private interaction creates desirability or reputation scores; or
- Safety information drives ordinary personalization.

Exclusion creates no authority to collect information for possible future use. It authorizes no speculative retention, shadow dataset, semantic summary, embedding, feature, model-learning copy, logging, or pipeline. Historical availability or legacy implementation cannot create future authority or bypass the future gate.
