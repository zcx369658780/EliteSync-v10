## 6. Two-layer proposition authority classification

A proposition may contain both a direct legal anchor and an application to EliteSync facts. Future Track C work must classify them separately.

Legal anchor classification:

- `PRIMARY LAW — DIRECT TEXT`
- `ADMINISTRATIVE REGULATION — DIRECT TEXT`
- `REGULATORY RULE — DIRECT TEXT`
- `OFFICIAL GUIDANCE / EXPLANATION`

EliteSync application classification:

- `AI INTERPRETATION`
- `AI INFERENCE`
- `LEGAL UNCERTAINTY`

Conceptual example:

- Legal anchor — personal information includes information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person: `PRIMARY LAW — DIRECT TEXT`.
- Application — a particular retained user-linked interpretation may fall within that definition: `AI INTERPRETATION` or `AI INFERENCE`, with factual uncertainty stated.

A second example:

- Legal anchor — supplied law contains good-faith, transparency, and personal-information quality requirements: `PRIMARY LAW — DIRECT TEXT`.
- Application — representing a user-linked AI interpretation as verified fact may create legal risk: `AI INTERPRETATION`, not verbatim statutory text.

## 7. Owner Q1 — User declarations

Owner outcome: `ACCEPTED`

A user declaration is authoritative as declaration and may be personal information when it relates to an identified or identifiable natural person. It is not thereby objective fact or future-behavior truth.

`User declaration ≠ objective truth`

LC-01 law is `SUPPORTED / CONSISTENT` with this principle, but does not legally mandate it as product epistemology.

## 8. Owner Q2 — Derived and AI interpretations

Owner outcome: `ACCEPTED`

A retained Compatibility, personality, or AI interpretation linked to an identified or identifiable user must be treated as potentially personal information unless later legal and factual analysis establishes otherwise.

This is an interpretive legal guardrail, not direct statutory wording. It does not make every derived interpretation automatically personal information. Classification remains fact-dependent, including identifiability, recording, linkage, anonymization, and actual information content.

## 9. Owner Q3 — Sensitive personal information

Owner outcome: `ACCEPTED`

Relationship preferences, personality answers, and Compatibility information are not automatically sensitive personal information. Sensitive status depends on actual content, actual context, applicable statutory category, and the relevant statutory harm-based definition.

Legal classification follows actual information content and processing context, not an internal product-field name. A field named relationship preference is not sensitive merely because of that name; its actual content may nevertheless fall within a statutory category or legal test.
