## 0. Scope, authority, and evidence discipline

This workshop proposes canonical current-MVP interaction-state meanings, qualitative non-coercive pacing invariants, richer-Relationship-stage exclusion, and strict numeric/configuration deferral. It creates no generic/global relationship-state field, product acceptance, participant or data activity, Relationship-status feature, empirical finding, configuration, technical design, or implementation.

### Observed / Accepted Authority

- Product Truth → Domain → Information → Architecture → Implementation remains the refinement order.
- Match ≠ Connection ≠ Conversation ≠ Relationship.
- A Match is a bounded introduction opportunity, not a score, mutual state, contact consent, or Relationship.
- Only reciprocal, current User choice creates Connection; Connection is revocable and grants bounded communication opportunity only.
- Conversation activity cannot infer Relationship, affection, permanence, success, Compatibility, or person truth.
- Relationship meaning and status remain human-defined and mutually understood, not platform-inferred from activity.
- `Informed Progression` is improved human decision quality under uncertainty, not a Relationship-progress state or state machine. Continuation is not automatically superior to pause, closure, or uncertainty.
- Pause and opt-out are distinct from Identity deletion. Pause, reset, close, expire, correction, revocation, and supersession retain distinct lifecycle force.
- Missingness, no response, expiry, pause, pass, and closure create no adverse person meaning.
- Safety evidence and case meaning remain isolated. Only minimum-necessary Safety control directives may cross into ordinary enforcement, scoped to the affected interaction or purpose.
- Storage, telemetry, historical state, AI output, labels, or implementation convenience creates no lifecycle, consent, Relationship, processing, or technical authority.

### Prior Conceptual Exploration

The Product Vision and User Journey workshops propose bounded supply and deliberate attention. They are conceptual exploration where not superseded. They do not validate pacing, select configuration values, prove user comprehension, authorize data activity, or establish implementation defaults. EliteSync 9.x values and competitor practices are historical/reference material only.

### Product Inference

Separate product-policy vocabularies can preserve the source and force of supply participation, opportunity lifecycle, unilateral declaration, reciprocal consent, communication access, ordinary lifecycle cessation, Safety control, and human Relationship meaning. Qualitative pacing constraints can prevent coercive product behavior without pretending exact values are known.

## 2. Introduction-supply participation vocabulary

### Option A — three purpose-scoped participation meanings (recommended)

- `RECEIVING INTRODUCTIONS`: the User currently permits future introduction supply for the named purpose. It does not establish Match Readiness, eligibility, candidate availability, priority, or guaranteed supply.
- `INTRODUCTIONS PAUSED`: the User stops new introduction supply while retaining Identity and already-authorized lifecycle rights. Pause is User control, not Identity deletion, negative evidence, punishment, or person meaning.
- `INTRODUCTIONS CLOSED`: the User ends future introduction participation for the named purpose only. It does not by itself delete Identity, account, history, or another person's independent rights; it implies no rejection motive, Relationship, wrongdoing, or Safety meaning.

Prefer purpose-specific terms over `active` or `available` where those labels could imply eligibility, readiness, supply, or person availability.

### Option B — no canonical introduction-supply participation vocabulary

Retain the affected scope blocked rather than use ambiguous `active/available` language.

Recommendation: A. Reject or exit A if receiving implies readiness/eligibility/supply, pause or close becomes Identity deletion, or any state creates negative standing or punishment.

## 3. Match opportunity vocabulary

### Option A — bounded opportunity lifecycle (recommended)

- `MATCH PRESENTED`: the product presented a bounded introduction opportunity. It creates no mutuality, contact consent, Connection, Relationship, or Compatibility proof.
- `INTEREST EXPRESSED`: one User affirmatively expressed interest. It is unilateral `User Declared` only, not mutuality, contact consent, a promise, or another User's position.
- `PASSED`: one User declined the opportunity. It is a neutral User choice and creates no adverse meaning.
- `EXPIRED`: the opportunity is no longer active under a separately authorized timing policy. Expiry is lifecycle cessation only; it does not establish motive, incompatibility, fault, rejection, or person meaning.

No one-sided state creates Connection.

### Option B — retain only the high-level Match concept

Do not use local lifecycle/action terms until their semantics can be preserved.

Recommendation: A. Reject or exit A if any state becomes a score, mutual state, contact consent, adverse signal, Relationship status, or inferred motive.

## 4. Connection vocabulary

### Option A — reciprocal and revocable Connection (recommended)

- `CONNECTION`: reciprocal, current, revocable agreement to explore communication.
- `CONNECTION CLOSED`: future ordinary communication access under that Connection ended through valid lifecycle authority.

Connection is not Relationship, friendship, commitment, exclusivity, permanence, off-platform consent, Compatibility proof, or consent to unrelated purposes. Closure is future-access cessation only.

## 8. Non-coercive pacing invariants

### Option A — qualitative invariants only (recommended)

- introduction supply remains bounded rather than effectively infinite;
- Users can pause or close future supply without deleting Identity;
- pacing supports deliberate consideration rather than throughput or session-time optimization;
- pause, pass, expiry, and no response do not trigger punishment, reduced person standing, or manipulative supply changes;
- no scarcity, fear-of-missing-out, guilt, streak, countdown, urgency, or escalating-pressure mechanics may force interaction;
- reminders, if separately authorized later, must be bounded and non-escalatory;
- engagement does not imply consent for more supply or pressure;
- a current pause or scoped restriction cannot be bypassed; and
- a minimum-necessary Safety directive may stop affected supply/access but creates no ordinary person meaning.

These are proposed product-policy invariants, not empirically validated behavioral findings.

### Option B — retain pacing policy fully blocked

Do not make a pacing promise beyond existing consent and Safety boundaries.
