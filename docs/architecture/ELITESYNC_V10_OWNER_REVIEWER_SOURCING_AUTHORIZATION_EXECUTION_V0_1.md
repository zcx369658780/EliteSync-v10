## 2. Allowed sourcing activities

The Owner-designated human sourcing lead may:

- use recognized professional bodies and their public professional directories;
- use public professional pages of research institutions;
- use public professional pages and directories of standards organizations;
- use other Owner-approved professional directories;
- use an exact Owner-approved referral as a sourcing lead without contacting the referrer or candidate;
- use an exact Owner-approved nomination;
- record minimal public professional and qualification information necessary to document discovery relevance for one or more authorized packages;
- record sourcing channel, exact public locator, date accessed, package linkage, and sourcing-only relevance terms;
- use the candidate-record structure and controls in Section 5.

Allowed channel categories do not authorize paid access, authentication, terms acceptance, scraping, automated enrichment, external disclosure, or contact. Any unlisted channel requires a later exact Owner revision before use.

## 3. Forbidden sourcing activities

Sourcing must not include:

- candidate evaluation, qualification verification as a decision, comparison, scoring, ranking, recommendation, selection, approval, rejection, or appointment;
- contacting or messaging a candidate, referrer, employer, institution, colleague, referee, professional body, or any other person;
- outreach, availability inquiry, opinion request, reference check, interview, negotiation, terms discussion, or work request;
- engagement, hiring, commissioning, contracting, compensation, reimbursement, gift, subscription/procurement spend, or financial commitment;
- project-material disclosure, corpus approval/access/transfer, restricted-source access, credential use, or production access;
- inferring personal, protected, sensitive, private, Safety, political, health, relationship, or other non-necessary traits;
- covert profiling, data brokers, scraped lists, bulk harvesting, automated outreach, private/social content discovery, or AI-generated candidate identities;
- specialist review, findings, legal opinion, compliance claim, Safety decision, case review, participant/data activity, technical design, implementation planning, or code.

Apparent relevance recorded during sourcing is a discovery note only. It is not a qualification finding or candidate evaluation.

## 4. Information and confidentiality boundary

### 4.1 Allowed information

Only the minimum public professional information necessary for sourcing:

- public professional name or identifier;
- current publicly stated professional role and organizational affiliation;
- public qualification, training, publication, standards, or professional-experience descriptors relevant to sourcing;
- public source title, issuer/organization, exact locator, publication/update date where available, and access date;
- exact authorized package linkage and neutral sourcing-relevance keywords;
- source provenance and `NOT EVALUATED` verification state.

### 4.2 Forbidden information

- personal data beyond sourcing necessity, including private contact details, home/family information, personal social activity, or inferred characteristics;
- sensitive, protected, health, political, biometric, financial, private-interaction, Safety, participant, real-case, allegation, emergency, production, credential, or confidential information;
- repository-private or confidential project material, evidence corpus content, user information, or unpublished findings;
- information obtained through deception, unauthorized access, scraping, data brokers, private accounts, or contact.

Public availability does not remove necessity, purpose, accuracy, confidentiality, retention, or deletion obligations.

### 4.3 Confidentiality boundary

Candidate records are confidential project-governance records even when their fields originate from public sources. They may not be published, shared externally, disclosed to a candidate, used for unrelated profiling, transferred into a specialist review corpus, or reused for another purpose/package without exact authority.

## 5. Candidate record governance

### 5.1 Existence and status

A candidate record may exist only after a potential candidate is discovered within this authorization. Every record must display:

`POTENTIAL CANDIDATE — UNVERIFIED — SOURCING-ONLY — NOT EVALUATED — NOT CONTACTED — NOT AUTHORIZED FOR ENGAGEMENT`.

Creation does not imply qualification, fit, ranking, recommendation, selection, availability, authority, consent, or willingness to participate.

### 5.2 Minimum candidate record

```text
Candidate record ID:
Status: POTENTIAL CANDIDATE — UNVERIFIED — SOURCING-ONLY — NOT EVALUATED — NOT CONTACTED
Linked package(s):
Discovery date:
Owner authorization ID: ORS-01
Authorized channel category:
Public source/locator and access date:
Minimum public professional identifier:
Public role/affiliation:
Public qualification descriptors relevant to sourcing:
Neutral sourcing-relevance note — NOT AN EVALUATION:
Information excluded/minimized:
Record creator:
Custodian:
Access list:
Retention/review/expiry state:
Correction/deletion history:
Stop/escalation events:
```

Do not include opinions, suitability conclusions, rankings, recommendations, private contact details, inferred traits, project materials, evidence findings, or outreach text.

### 5.3 Custodian and access

The Owner must assign an accountable human sourcing custodian before records are created. Access is least-privilege and limited to the assigned sourcing lead, custodian, Owner, and any explicitly named governance verifier. No candidate, external party, specialist, technical team, AI service, or later-stage role receives access by default. Access does not authorize evaluation or contact.

### 5.4 Retention and review

Retain only while the sourcing authorization and documented sourcing purpose remain active and necessary. The custodian must periodically review necessity, source accuracy, authorization state, access list, and stop events. No default indefinite archive is authorized. Retention does not authorize evaluation, contact, engagement, or reuse.

### 5.5 Correction and deletion

Correct objective source/identity errors without adding evaluation. Delete a record and derived copies when authorization expires or is revoked, sourcing purpose is complete, necessity ends, a stop condition requires deletion, source provenance fails, or the Owner directs deletion. Record only a minimal deletion audit event—record ID, date, responsible human, and reason category—without preserving unnecessary candidate data.

### 5.6 Candidate-record stop conditions

Stop creation, update, access, or retention if authorization, package, channel, provenance, necessity, accuracy, confidentiality, access, custodian, retention, or expiry is unclear; personal/sensitive/restricted information would be needed; a conflict or independence concern appears that would require evaluation; or anyone requests ranking, selection, contact, availability, opinion, project material, negotiation, engagement, spend, evidence transfer, or review execution.

## 6. Package sourcing overlays

### 6.1 SRA-RM-01 — Research methodology

- **Sourcing relevance:** Public professional evidence of research methodology expertise, evidence-quality assessment, validity, measurement, evidence synthesis, uncertainty, or research design.
- **Neutral sourcing note only:** Record public descriptors and source locators; do not decide competence, evidence quality, methodological correctness, or qualification.
- **Excluded:** Participant research execution, data/dataset/statistical analysis, experiment, model, metric, threshold, signal approval, or technical work.

### 6.2 SRA-PL-01 — Privacy/legal

- **Sourcing relevance:** Public professional evidence related to privacy, information rights, or data governance.
- **Neutral sourcing note only:** Record public descriptors and source locators; do not decide jurisdictional competence, qualification, legal authority, or suitability.
- **Excluded:** Selecting or inferring jurisdiction, population, entity, purpose, or lawful basis; seeking legal advice/opinion; compliance/legality claim; personal/restricted-data activity; technical work.

### 6.3 SRA-SM-01 — Safety/moderation

- **Sourcing relevance:** Public professional evidence related to Safety/moderation, responsibility boundaries, escalation, human review, recourse, or harm-aware conceptual policy.
- **Neutral sourcing note only:** Record public descriptors and source locators; do not decide Safety competence, authority, case-handling ability, or qualification.
- **Excluded:** Case/report/allegation/emergency review, Safety decision, guilt/reputation judgment, person-level assessment/risk scoring, moderation/restriction action, external routing, Safety/private data, or technical work.

Package linkage may be multiple only when separate public sourcing descriptors support each linkage. Shared linkage does not merge package scopes or authorize cross-package evaluation.

## 7. AI-assisted sourcing boundary

AI/tool outputs remain `AI-assisted research preparation`.

### 7.1 Allowed AI assistance

- organize the Owner-approved sourcing criteria and channel list;
- prepare blank sourcing/candidate-record templates;
- normalize formatting of human-collected public-source notes without adding facts;
- document exact public locators and provenance supplied by the human sourcing lead;
- run completeness/consistency checks on candidate records.

### 7.2 Forbidden AI activity

- independently discover or identify the final specialist;
- act as sourcing lead, custodian, qualification verifier, candidate evaluator, specialist, or Owner authority;
- infer or verify authority alone; rank, score, recommend, approve, reject, or select candidates;
- enrich candidate information, infer sensitive traits, scrape private/public sources, or use unapproved search channels;
- contact candidates or other people, request availability/opinions, negotiate, or represent Owner authority;
- convert public information into evidence certification, specialist findings, or later-stage authorization.

Material AI assistance retained in a record must identify the tool/model/version where disclosed, purpose, prompt/context boundary, human reviewer, limitations, and verification state. Human review does not promote AI output to qualification evidence or candidate evaluation.
